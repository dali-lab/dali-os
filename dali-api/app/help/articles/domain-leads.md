If you lead a domain, the **Domain** tab under [Hiring](/hiring) is your page for the cycle. Each hiring cycle runs on Core's schedule, and your part is to get your domain ready, staff reviews and interviews, and decide who moves on. If you lead several domains, pick one with the **Domain** picker at the top. A second picker switches between the current cycle and past ones.

If you don't lead a domain yet, the page says "You don't lead a domain yet."

## What you see

The page is a set of sections along the side. Which ones appear depends on the cycle's stage.

- **Challenges** (cycles that use them): while the cycle is in Draft or open and not yet ready, add or link a challenge form with **Add challenge form**, then click **Mark as ready**. A ready domain shows **Ready**. While the cycle is still in Draft you can reopen it for edits with **Edit challenges**. Once the cycle is open, a ready domain can't be unmarked. Core can't open applications until every domain is ready.
- **Rubric**: shows **Set** or **Not set**. For cycles with challenges, you pick the domain's rubric from the dropdown. It locks once reviews exist. Reviewers can only be assigned after the hiring lead sets the general rubric.
- **Reviewers**: who reads your domain's applications. Add people from the roster card. Removing a reviewer who already submitted deletes that review, and you are asked to confirm.
- **Interviewers** (cycles with interviews): your pool, with how many have free time on their calendar. A domain with no free time shows a **No free time** warning, and people with no calendar connected show **No calendar**.
- **Reviews**: a table of your applicants with reviewer progress and decisions.
- **Deliberations**: one row per round, with **Start**, **Continue** or **Reopen** and a count of applicants who qualify.
- **Interviews**: who has been invited, who has booked, and what is scheduled or completed.

## Assign reviewers

In **Reviews**, click **Auto-assign reviewers** to spread applications across your reviewers. It needs a rubric and at least one reviewer. If applications are still coming in, run it again after the close to cover the late ones. You can also assign reviewers per application from the table row actions. Reviewers then see their cards on their own [My work](/hiring) page.

## Run deliberations

Deliberations appear once the cycle is Under Review. Each round is a separate board.

1. In **Deliberations**, click **Start** for the round. The board opens with every qualifying application in the first column.
2. Drag applications between columns. The columns depend on the round: a round that leads to interviews has **No Decision**, **Interview** and **Reject**, a middle round has **No Decision**, **Advance** and **Reject**, and the final round has **Accept**, **Waitlist** and **Reject**. In the final round everyone starts in **Accept**, so move each person who should not be accepted. Changes save as you go, and reviewers watch a live read-only copy.
3. Click a card to see the full application and all reviews. On rounds that lead to interviews, write the **Interview Prep Note** here. Interviewers see it on their interview page.
4. When you are done, click **Close delibs** and confirm. Closing creates draft decisions. In earlier rounds, anyone left in **No Decision** gets no decision, and **Advance** creates no decision either: the closed board is what qualifies those people for the next round.

A closed round can be reopened from the same row.

## Interviews and finalizing

Draft decisions from a round that leads to interviews become interview invites. The hiring lead (Core) releases invites and sends the emails, and applicants then book a time. In **Interviews** you can follow who has booked and who has not.

After the final round, drafts need a second step from you. Under **Interviews** or in the **Reviews** table, use the **Needs finalization** filter and click **Finalize all**, or finalize one applicant at a time. Finalizing locks the decision and hands it to the hiring lead, who releases it to the applicant. Nothing is emailed until Core releases it.

## Blind review

If the cycle has blind review on, your applicants show as "Applicant N" until you send interview invites or close your first delib. After that, names appear for your domain.

## When something looks wrong

- Reviewer assignment is greyed out: set the rubric, then add reviewers.
- Rubric or reviewer can't be changed: it is locked once reviews exist. Ask the hiring lead.
- Someone's interviews are not showing: check that the cycle has interviews and that the person has connected a calendar.
