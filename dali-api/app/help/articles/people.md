People is the lab directory. It lists every active member and, on a second view, every alum. Each row opens that person's profile.

## Find someone

Open [People](/members) from the sidebar (under **Lab**).

- Use the **Active** and **Alumni** switch to change who is listed. Alumni are sorted by class year, newest first.
- Type in the search box to match a name or an email address.
- Use the filter bar to narrow the list by **Domain**, **Project**, **Class year**, **Role**, **Major**, **Based in**, **Company** or **Hometown**. A person with several domains or projects matches any one of them. Project filters include anyone who was ever on that project, not only this term.
- Filters and search are saved in the page address, so you can send a filtered view to someone.

Links from other pages, such as a group's "Open in People directory" button, open the directory with a domain or project filter already applied.

## Open a profile

Click a row, or go to your own profile at [My profile](/profile), which takes you to your member page. A profile has up to three tabs, **Profile**, **Activity** and **Mentorship**.

At the top you see the photo, name, @handle, and badges such as **Staff**, **Alumni**, a graduate program, **New**, and a birthday badge on the day. A small dot and line show when the person was last active ("Active now" if seen in the last minute). People can hide it with the **Appear away** switch in their settings.

### Profile tab

Cards on this tab:

- **About**: pronouns, handle, NetID, major, class year, hometown, based in, birthday and dietary restrictions.
- **Contact**: email, phone, personal site, time zone, GitHub username and LinkedIn URL.
- **Roles, domains & levels**: Core or lead roles and the domains the person is eligible for, with their level. Only Core or Admin can change eligibility levels.
- **Groups**: the groups the person is in. Only Core, Admin and Instructors see this card. See [Member groups](/help/articles/groups).
- **Work experience**: jobs, current ones first.
- **Membership pass**: shown on someone else's profile only to Core and Admin, who can reset or revoke a lost or shared wallet pass.

### Activity tab

Achievements, project history grouped by term, and pending reviews. On your own profile it reads **My activity**. Core and the member themselves also see education history (taught, CE credits, attended) and compliance status.

### Mentorship tab

Appears only when you are viewing someone else and you are Core, Admin or a lab mentor. It lists that person's mentorship pairings and links to their notes. See [Mentorship](/help/articles/mentorship).

## Edit a profile

You can edit your own profile. Admins can edit anyone's.

1. Open the profile and click **Edit** on the **About** or **Contact** card.
2. Change the fields and click **Save**, or **Cancel** to discard.
3. To add a job, click **Add** on **Work experience**. Use **Edit** or **Remove** on an existing job.

The photo is changed by clicking it on a profile you can edit. "Based in" feeds the Community map for alumni, so keep it current. See [Community](/help/articles/community-map).

## Ask for a resume review

When a member has turned on resume reviews, a **Resume review** button shows on their profile. Click it and confirm with **Send request**. They get a notification and see your name only if they accept. See [Community](/help/articles/community-map).

## Add a new member

Core and Admin see a **New member** button on the People page. Fill in first name, last name and email (required), plus optional Dartmouth email, class year and profile fields, then click **Create**. If the person already exists, they are promoted to a member instead of duplicated.

## Who can see what

- Every lab member and alum can open the directory and profiles.
- Applicants and partners are sent to their own portals instead.
- Education history and compliance on a profile are limited to the member and Core.
