Education is where the lab runs its workshops, miniseries and fellowships. You find an offering, apply or RSVP, attend its sessions, do any assignments, and get a certificate when you finish.

## Offering types

- **Workshop**: one session.
- **Miniseries**: several sessions in one term.
- **Fellowship**: several sessions across more than one term.

Each offering is either reviewed or RSVP. On a reviewed offering you click **Apply** and an instructor decides. On an RSVP offering you click **RSVP** and are approved automatically while seats last, then go on a waitlist. Workshops are usually RSVP, and miniseries and fellowships are usually reviewed.

## Your Education page

[Education](/education) is your home for all of this. It shows your courses, what is coming up next, work that is due, and your applications with a status next to each (Submitted, Approved, Waitlisted, Rejected or Withdrawn). Once a course has issued your certificate, a **Certificate** link appears next to it.

Near the top, a **CE credit** line shows whether you have earned your credit for the term. See [Continuing education credits](/help/articles/ce-credits).

If you are not in a course yet, the page offers a **Browse offerings** button.

## Find an offering

Open [Offerings](/education/offerings) to see every published offering. Open one to see its dates, registration window, seats left, who teaches it, a description and its session schedule. Past sessions fade so the next one stands out.

## Apply or RSVP

1. Open the offering and click **Apply** (for reviewed offerings) or **RSVP** (for offerings that approve automatically).
2. Answer the questions and click **Submit application** or **RSVP**.
3. You come back to the offering page, where your status shows.

The button only appears while registration is open. If you have already been approved, waitlisted or rejected, you cannot apply again. While your application is still **Submitted**, the button reads **Edit application** and you can change your answers until registration closes.

To back out, click **Withdraw** on the offering page and confirm. If you were approved, your seat goes to the next person on the waitlist. You can re-apply after withdrawing while registration is still open. You get a notification when your application is decided, and an email if one is set up for that decision.

## Take the course

Once you are approved, click **Open course hub** on the offering page (or open the course from your Education page). The hub has four tabs:

- **Overview**: the course description, any feedback your instructor left you, and your certificate once you have it.
- **Sessions**: every session with its materials, assignments and your attendance, and how many sessions you have attended so far. A badge on this tab counts assignments you still need to turn in.
- **Discussions**: post a question or reply to the class, and read announcements from your instructors. Announcements also notify everyone enrolled, by email depending on their notification settings.
- **People**: your instructors and classmates.

## Attendance

An instructor marks each person **Present**, **Absent** or **Excused**. Instructors can also open self check-in for a session. While it is open, a **Check in** button appears on that session in your hub, and scanning the projected QR code takes you to a check-in page where you click **Check in**. You must be signed in and approved for the course. Check-in stays open until the instructor closes it, and automatically closes about 30 minutes after the session ends.

A Present mark also earns you a CE credit for the term.

## Assignments

Open an assignment from its session in the hub. What you do depends on its type:

- **Text**, **File** or **Text + files**: write your answer or upload files, then **Submit**. If you already submitted, the button reads **Resubmit**.
- **Link / URL**: paste a link and **Submit**.
- **Document (in-app)**: write in a shared doc that saves as you type, then click **Turn in**. Your doc is only recorded as turned in once you click it. Click **Re-turn in** to update it.
- **Mark complete only**: click **Mark complete**.

You cannot submit after the due date. When an instructor releases a grade, it shows on the assignment with any score and feedback.

## Certificates

When an instructor closes out a course, everyone who met the attendance requirement gets a certificate and a notification. For a workshop you need to have been present. For a miniseries or fellowship, present plus excused sessions must reach the requirement set for that course (80 percent unless the instructor changed it). Open the certificate from **View certificate** on the hub Overview or from your Education page. On the certificate page you can **Download PDF** or **Print**.

## Dartmouth students

Students who are not lab members use the same flow at a separate address under `/portal/education`. Signing in takes you there automatically.
