New alumni get a playful way to settle in. The first time you sign in, DALI OS invites you to finish your alumni profile, and **Walk the pixel lab** does it inside a small game set in the lab. You build a pixel avatar first, then choose between the lab and **Fill in my profile**, the three-step form. Finishing either one counts.

**Finish later** in the top corner of the avatar page takes you back to DALI OS.

## Build your avatar

On [Welcome back](/alumni/welcome), pick a skin color, eye color and hairstyle. You can come back and change it later with **Edit my avatar**.

## Walk around the lab

Choose **Enter the lab** to drop your avatar into a pixel version of the DALI lab. Move with the arrow keys or WASD and press **E** to interact with what is near you:

- Open the fridge, the cabinets and the pizza box, and peek into the APPA and MOMO pods.
- Talk to Tim and Lorie, the two people standing in the lab.

Scroll to zoom in, and drag with the scroll wheel pressed down to look around the lab. The view follows your avatar again once you move.

Use **Leave the lab** in the top corner to go back to DALI OS.

## Tim's quest

Talk to Tim and accept his quest to open a phone on the side of the screen. Tim asks about the same things as the profile form, one question at a time, and each answer is saved straight to your profile. He confirms your name and the email you want to be reached at (shown as **Primary** on your profile), then asks about your work now and before that, your majors and minors, a fun fact, what students can ask you about, what you do outside of work, your birthday, and what you are open to: coffee chats, resume or portfolio reviews, mentoring a project, speaking at a lab event and partner referrals. He also shows you the DALI roles and projects on file. If something in that history looks wrong, tell him in the chat and Core gets a notification to fix it.

The bar at the top of the phone shows how complete your profile is. You can **Skip** any question except your name and email, and fill the rest in on your [profile](/profile) later. When the questions run out, your profile counts as finished and the invite stops showing. Use **View my profile** on the phone to see everything Tim saved.
