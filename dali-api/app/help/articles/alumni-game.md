New alumni get a playful way to settle in. When the alumni welcome flow is on, it is the first thing you see after you sign in, and it ends with a link to the pixel lab. Otherwise, the first time you sign in, DALI OS invites you to build a pixel avatar of yourself. After that you can finish your alumni profile the normal way on your [profile](/profile), or do it inside a small game set in the lab.

## Build your avatar

On [Welcome back](/alumni/welcome), pick a skin color, eye color and hairstyle. You can come back and change it later with **Edit my avatar**.

## Walk around the lab

Choose **Enter the lab** to drop your avatar into a pixel version of the DALI lab. Move with the arrow keys or WASD and press **E** to interact with what is near you:

- Open the fridge, the cabinets and the pizza box, and peek into the APPA and MOMO pods.
- Talk to Tim and Lorie, the two people standing in the lab.

Scroll to zoom in, and drag with the scroll wheel pressed down to look around the lab. The view follows your avatar again once you move.

Use **Leave the lab** in the top corner to go back to DALI OS.

## Tim's quest

Talk to Tim and accept his quest to open a phone on the side of the screen. Tim asks about the same things as the profile form, one question at a time, and each answer is saved straight to your profile. He confirms your name, the email you want to be reached at (shown as **Primary** on your profile), your birthday, and shows you the DALI roles and projects we have on file. If something in that history looks wrong, just tell him in the chat and it gets flagged for the team to fix.

The bar at the top of the phone shows how complete your profile is. You can **Skip** any question and finish the rest on your [profile](/profile) later. When the questions run out, use **View my complete profile** on the phone to double check everything landed where you expect.

## The quick form

Prefer to type it all in at once? [Fill in your profile](/alumni/form) covers the same checklist as the quest: your name, primary email, class year, birthday, where you are based, work, links, dietary restrictions, the DALI history check, and what you are open to (coffee chats, resume and portfolio reviews). Saving takes you to your profile so you can double check it.
