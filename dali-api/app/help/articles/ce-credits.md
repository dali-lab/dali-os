Every lab member is expected to earn at least one continuing education (CE) credit per term. You earn it by attending a session of an education offering. Nothing is blocked if you miss it. It is tracked so Core can follow up.

## How you earn a credit

- An instructor marks you **Present** at a session of any workshop, miniseries or fellowship. That grants one credit for the term the session falls in.
- If your mark is changed to **Absent**, **Excused** or cleared, the credit from that session goes away. Excused does not earn a credit.
- Instructors get a credit for the term when the offering they teach is closed out.
- Core can also grant a credit by hand, with a reason, for cases like completing the async CEC check-in.

One credit is enough for the term. Attending more sessions adds more credits but does not change your standing.

## Check your standing

Open [Education](/education). The **CE credit** line under the title says either how many credits you have earned for the term, or that you have 0 of 1 and should attend a session to earn one.

The line does not appear if there is no current term, or if you are full-time staff. Staff are exempt from the requirement.

## If you are short

Sign up for an offering, or attend a session of one you are already in. See [Education](/help/articles/education) for how to find and join an offering. If you did a different qualifying activity, ask Core to grant the credit. When you are still missing a credit, Core can send you a reminder as an in-app notification, and an email depending on your notification settings.

## Who sees compliance across the lab

Only Core can see the lab-wide compliance list. See [CE compliance](/help/articles/ce-compliance).
