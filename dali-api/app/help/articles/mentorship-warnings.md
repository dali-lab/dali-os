Mentorship warnings are where Core keeps track of participation concerns about members. Only Core can see them. Mentors and mentees can't.

## Open the Warnings tab

Open [Mentorship](/mentorship/browse) and choose the **Warnings** tab, next to **Notes**. The list shows each warning with the member, the reason, the date and whether it is open or resolved. Search by member name, or use **All**, **Open** and **Resolved** to narrow the list.

## Add a warning

1. Choose **Add warning** at the right of the tab row.
2. Pick the member, the reason and the date. The reasons are **Missed mentorship meeting**, **Missed lab** and **Other**.
3. Add notes. Notes are required when the reason is **Other** and optional otherwise.
4. Choose **Save warning**. New warnings start as open.

## Review, edit and resolve

Click a warning to open it. From there you can:

- Choose **Edit** to change the member, reason, notes or date.
- Choose **Mark resolved** when the concern is dealt with, or **Reopen** to bring it back.
- Choose **Delete** to remove it for good. This can't be undone.

## See open warnings on the Notes tab

Members with open warnings appear in an **Open warnings** row above the notes grid, with the latest reason and a count when there is more than one. Click a name or **Manage warnings** to go to the Warnings tab.
