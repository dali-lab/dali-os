The directory is the address book for partners. It holds organizations, individual partners and the people who work at them, whether or not they have applied. Only Core can see it. Applications live on the [Partners board](/help/articles/partners).

## Browse the directory

Open [Partners](/core/partners), then **Directory**. Each row is an organization, an individual partner, or a person. Search by name, or filter by kind, type, status and organization. **Export CSV** downloads the rows that match your search and filters.

An organization's status is worked out for you:

- **Active**: at least one project partnership is still open.
- **Past**: every partnership has ended.
- **Prospect**: no project partnerships yet.
- **Dormant**: Past, and quiet for a year.

Organization types are Dartmouth department, faculty research, startup, nonprofit, company, alumni and other.

Click **New** and choose **Organization**, **Individual partner** or **Contact**. A name is required. An individual partner and a contact also need an email address. Adding a contact whose email already exists opens the existing contact instead of making a duplicate.

## An organization

Open a row to see the organization page. Its tabs are:

- **Timeline**: everything that has happened, including applications, emails and invoices.
- **Contacts**: members of the organization. **Add existing contact** attaches someone already in the directory. **Invite member** emails an invitation to the partner portal. A person shows **Invited** until they accept, and **Revoke invite** cancels it. Each member can have a role such as CTO. **Set primary** picks the main contact. **Move** sends a member to another organization. **Remove** takes them off. An individual partner has one contact instead, with no invites.
- **Applications**: the organization's applications, with their stages.
- **Projects**: funded projects. **Link project** ties the organization to a project. You can later end the partnership, which keeps the history, or unlink it. The organization shows Active while a link is open.
- **Finance**: deal terms from accepted applications, chart strings, and **Invoices**.
- **Settings**: name, type, address, legal entity name, tags, notes, website and whether the partner agreed to be showcased. The Individual checkbox is for a person partnering on their own. Here you can also merge or delete.

For an individual partner, the **Contacts** tab has **Send sign-in link** (or **Resend sign-in link** once they have signed in), which emails a one-time link to the partner portal. Only a few can be sent in a short time.

## Invoices

On the **Finance** tab, add an invoice with an amount (required), reference, issued date, due date and note. Set its status to Draft, Issued, Paid or Void. Marking an invoice Issued or Paid adds an entry to the organization's timeline and fills in the date if it is empty.

## Merge or delete an organization

Under **Settings**, **Merge into another organization** moves members, projects, applications and history into the one you choose. Use it when the same partner was entered twice.

**Delete organization** only works when the organization has no members, project links, applications or pending invites. Deleting can't be undone.

## A contact

Open a person to see their details: name, email, title, phone, LinkedIn, affiliation and preferred channel. The tabs show their timeline, applications, meetings, email threads with them, and details. Add an internal note on the timeline to keep context for the next person who talks to them.
