Many projects are built for an outside partner, such as a department, nonprofit or company. Partners have their own portal where they follow the project. As a project member, you decide what goes there.

## Preview the partner view

On a project page, use the view switch at the top and choose **Partner view**. It shows what the partner sees, and any lab member can open it. Choose **Internal view** to go back.

The partner view has these sections:

- **Roadmap**: the project's epics and stories on a timeline. Partners can open an item to read its description.
- **Drive**: the documents and files you have shared, grouped in their folders. If nothing is shared, it says the team hasn't shared any documents or files yet.
- **Team**: the people staffed on the project this term, with their domains. It only shows when someone is staffed.
- **Meetings**: meetings with the partner. This section only shows for a real partner signed in to their portal. The preview leaves it out.

## Share a document or file with the partner

Nothing is shared by default. Anyone on the project team (and Core) can share.

1. Open the project and find the document or file in its Drive section.
2. Open the item's menu and choose **Share with partner**.
3. Confirm **Share**. Members of the partner organization can view it right away.

A small icon marks items that are shared. To stop, open the menu again and choose **Stop sharing with partner**.

A folder appears in the partner's Drive only when something inside it is shared, and it shows only the shared items. Think of the partner view as a read-only window. Partners can see what you share but can't edit it.

Check the **Partner view** after you share to make sure it looks right.

## Meetings with the partner

Core schedules interviews and other meetings with the partner. Partners can also ask for a meeting from their portal by picking a time from the availability of the people involved. Core accepts or declines those requests, and an accepted request becomes a calendar event for the team. If you are invited, it shows up on your calendar like any other meeting.

Partner decisions such as contracts, the statement of work and fees are handled by Core. See [Partner applications](/help/articles/partners). If you need something changed there, ask Core.
