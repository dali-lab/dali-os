CE compliance shows which current members have earned their continuing education credit for a term. Only Core can open it, from **CE Compliance** in the Education sidebar section at [CE compliance](/education/compliance). Anyone else who opens the address is sent back to Education. The page only reports; nothing is blocked for a member who is missing a credit.

The rule is one credit per member per term. For how members earn credits, see [Continuing education credits](/help/articles/ce-credits).

## Read the list

Use the term filter at the top right to pick a term. If you choose all terms, the page shows the newest term. Each row is a current member with a pill showing their credit count: green when they have at least one, amber at zero. The line above the list says how many members are in the list and how many have no credit. Full-time staff are left out because they are exempt.

## See a member's history

Click a member's name to expand their history. It lists each credit, the term, and where it came from: a session they attended, or a manual grant with the name of who granted it.

## Grant a credit by hand

1. Click the member's name to expand them.
2. Type a reason, such as "Completed async CEC check-in". A reason is required.
3. Click **Grant credit**.

The credit is added to the term you are viewing. Manual grants are logged. Credits that came from attendance cannot be removed here. They go away when the attendance mark is changed in the offering's roster.

## Remind members

When anyone is missing a credit, a **Remind N non-compliant** button appears. Click it and confirm with **Send reminders**. Everyone without a credit for that term gets an in-app notification, plus an email depending on their notification preferences. The page tells you how many members were reminded.
