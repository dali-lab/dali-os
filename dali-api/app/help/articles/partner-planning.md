These pages sit next to the partner board in Core. Open [Partners](/core/partners) and use the tabs across the top: **Applications**, **Renewals**, **Planning**, **Directory**, **Referrals** and **Reports**. Only Core can see them.

## Renewals

[Renewals](/core/partners/renewals) is a board of existing projects with a partner, for deciding who continues in the next term. Pick a term at the top. The board shows the decision for the term after it. If there is no later term yet, it says renewals cannot be decided.

Cards sit in four columns: **Undecided**, **Continuing**, **Maintaining** and **Not continuing**.

- **Maintaining** is for projects that don't continue as term work but go onto DALI's recurring maintenance plan. Click **Maintain** on a card, or drag it there, then pick the plan: **Hosting**, **Mild** or **Medium**. The fee fills in from that plan's price and you can change it. The plan starts the next term and lasts until you move the card to another column, so these projects stay in Maintaining whichever term you pick. Moving a card out of Maintaining ends its plan.
- Set each plan's price per term in **Partner settings** (the gear on the Applications board), under **Maintenance prices**.
- Maintained projects don't count toward spots on Planning. Their tier also shows on the project page and on the partner's page in the Directory.

- Click **Continue** or **Not continuing** on a card to record the decision.
- When you continue a project, set the **Term**, the **Spots per domain** (use the fewer and more buttons), the **Fee**, and a next step. When you choose Not continuing, a **Reason** is required.
- To skip terms, continue the project and pick a later **Term**. The card shows "Continuing from" that term, the terms in between count as not continuing, and the project is planned again from the term you picked.
- You can change a decision later. Picking a different term, choosing Not continuing, or moving the card back to Undecided removes the term you picked before, along with its spots, unless members are already staffed on it. If that term has a PI, the term stays on the project but its spots are removed.
- Each card also has a **PI** picker and a next step with a date. An overdue next step is highlighted.
- Search by project or partner name.

Renewal decisions feed the planning page.

## Principal investigators

A principal investigator (PI) is the Core member responsible for a partner project in a term. A PI must be a Core member. You can set it on an application's side panel, on a renewal card, in a project's popup on the planning page, and on the project's term. The PI belongs to the term, so a project can have a different PI next term.

## Planning

[Planning](/core/partners/planning) shows how many team spots partner work will need in a term. Use the term picker to change term. It opens grouped by **Project**, with each project and application on its own. Group by **Domain** to see the spots wanted in each domain next to the number of members who said they intend to work, and the gap between them. The domain view needs an intent to work form bound to the term, and it says so when one isn't set up. It reads each member's latest answer to that form, the same answers the Intent to Work page shows, and counts someone in every role they hold. The **Total** counts once per role, with the number of distinct people next to it. By default only decided work counts: continuing renewals and accepted applications. **Include potential** also counts undecided renewals and applications still in New, Interview or Deciding. **Include backlog** adds backlog applications that target the term. Projects on a maintenance plan are listed under **Maintaining** at the bottom of the Project view and are not counted.

Click a project to open it without leaving the page. You can edit its description, and for each upcoming term set the PI and the spots it needs in each domain. Add a domain with **+ Domain**, use the minus and plus buttons to change how many spots, then click **Save** for that term. Mentor and other higher-level spots a project already has stay as they are; new spots are added as learner spots, and spots you remove come off the learner spots first.

The popup also has **Planning notes**: a short status for what's going on with the project right now. Notes show only on the planning page, not on the project page, and the popup says who last edited them. Clear the text and save to remove a note. When an application is turned into a project, its planning notes move to the new project.

Potential and backlog work opens the same kind of popup, so you can read it and assign a PI before it is confirmed. Click an application, at any stage, to see its summary, the terms it wants, its PI, its expected spots per domain and its planning notes, with a link to its card on the Partners board. You can edit all of them there, and the PI and spots carry over when it becomes a project. An undecided renewal shows the term it last ran, with that term's spots. The PI you set there carries forward if the project continues; decide its terms and spots in Renewals.

## Returning projects

Renewals covers projects running now. For a past project that may come back, because it got funding or something got unblocked, click **Start a return** on the planning page. Pick the project and the terms it would run. This adds an application to the partner board in **New**, with the same partner organization and contact, and last term's spots as its expected members. It shows on Planning with "(return)" after its stage, and its popup links to the past project.

The return moves through the board like any application. When you accept it, the button says **Reopen project**: instead of creating a new project, it adds the target terms back to the same project, so its history, files and planning notes stay in one place. Terms that already have spots keep them. If the partner is starting something fundamentally new, use a regular application instead, which creates a new project.

A project can have one open return at a time.

## Reports

[Reports](/core/partners/reports) answers questions about the pipeline for a term, or all terms, that you pick at the top:

- **Funnel**: how many applications reached each stage.
- **Where applications came from**: apply form, email, referral, manual or renewal.
- **Time to decision**: from applied to accepted, and from accepted to a project being created.
- **Why applications were rejected**: by reason.
- **New and returning partners**.
- **Fees by organization**: issued and paid invoices, totaled by organization.

## Referrals

[Referrals](/core/partners/referrals) lists the leads members submitted through the Refer a partner page. Search by partner, email or referrer. Open one to see everything the referrer wrote, add **Core notes**, and click **Mark as reached out** so the team can see what is still untouched. Nothing links a referral to an application automatically. See [Refer a partner](/help/articles/refer-a-partner) for what members see.
