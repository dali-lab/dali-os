DALI asks members to sign agreements such as the member agreement each term or the mentor agreement. This article covers signing as a member. Core people who create and track agreements should read [Agreements](/help/articles/agreements).

## When you are asked to sign

You get a notification when Core puts an agreement in force for a group you are in. It shows in the app, and it is also emailed unless you turned that off in your notification settings. If an agreement is required to use the app and you haven't signed it, DALI OS sends you to the signing page the next time you open any other page. After you sign, you land back where you were going.

Full-time staff and alumni are not asked to sign.

## Find what you owe

Open [Documents to sign](/sign). Each row is one agreement you still need to sign. The button on the right says **Review & sign**, or **Countersign** if you are signing as a mentee after your mentor.

If nothing is waiting, the page says you're all caught up.

## Sign a document

1. Open the agreement from [Documents to sign](/sign), or from the link in the email.
2. Read the document. Fields you need to fill are marked in the text: signature, initials, date, text boxes and checkboxes. The date fills in for you.
3. A progress pill at the bottom of the screen shows how many required fields you have done. Click **Start signing** or **Next field** to jump to the next empty field.
4. When every required field is done, click **I agree and sign**. If a field is still empty, the button takes you to it instead of signing.

Terms like the term name, your name and today's date are filled into the text for you.

## After you sign

You see a confirmation page with the signed copy. Click **Continue** to go back to where you were, or **Download PDF** to save the copy.

A receipt email goes out with a link, **View signed copy**, that opens the same page. You can also find old signed documents on your own [profile](/profile): open the **Activity** tab and look under **Agreements signed**, then select one to open its signed copy.

## Mentor agreements and countersigning

Some mentor agreements also need the mentee to sign.

- If you are a mentor and you have mentees, signing sends each of your mentees a request to countersign. Your receipt waits until your mentees have countersigned, so the copy you get has both signatures. Each time a mentee countersigns, you get their co-signed copy by email.
- If you are a mentee, your request says your mentor has already signed. Review the agreement and add your signature. You are emailed the co-signed copy.
- A mentor with no mentees gets their receipt right away.

## Things to know

- You can't sign the same version twice. If Core publishes a new version, you are asked to sign that one.
- You can only open signing pages for agreements that apply to you. A link to one that doesn't apply to you sends you back to the home page, and a link that no longer exists sends you to **Documents to sign**.
- If you can't sign something you think you should, or a document looks wrong, tell Core. Only Core can change or reissue an agreement.
