Core runs hiring from the **Hiring** area. The **Cycles**, **Waitlists** and **Onboarding** tabs are Core-only. **Library** is for Core and admins, and **Drive folders** is Core-only. Domain leads and reviewers use other tabs; see [Leading a domain in hiring](/help/articles/domain-leads) and [Reviewing and interviewing applicants](/help/articles/hiring).

## Create a cycle

1. Open [Cycles](/hiring/lead) and click **New cycle**.
2. Enter a **Name**, pick a **Term** and choose the **Applicants** group (for example Students, Interns or Lab members).
3. The new cycle opens in Draft. The term sets the dates: the default application window opens at the start of week 4 and closes at the end of week 5, and you can edit both dates.

Only an admin can create a Lab members cycle. Core creates the others.

## Set up in Draft

The cycle page has a tab per phase: Setup, Review, any Interviews and delib rounds, and Decisions. A cycle with a custom shape can add rounds in **Timeline**. Phase tabs show a check when their tasks are done. In **Setup**:

- **Term and dates**: the term, open and close dates, and when offers and waitlist responses are due.
- **Audience**, **Domains** and **General application**: the domains in the cycle (domain leads set up their own details on their Domain tab), and the application form. Cycles that use challenges require them per domain.
- **Continued interest** (Students): a form returning waitlisters fill out to reuse their application.
- **Application events**: hiring events applicants can attend, with check-in.
- **Blind review**: switch it **On** or **Off**. When on, everyone sees "Applicant N" until a domain sends interviews or closes its first delib.
- **Confidentiality agreement**: bind an agreement so reviewers must sign before seeing applicants.
- **Decision emails** and **Notification emails**: the email each decision or notice sends. A decision can't be released until its email is written.

When the close date is set, every domain is ready and the application form is added, click **Open applications** at the top. To stop taking submissions, click **Close applications**, which moves the cycle to Under Review. Extend the close date later with **Deadline extension** on the Setup tab.

## Staff the cycle

On the **Review** tab, add people under **Reviewers** (or **Reviewer pool** for member cycles) and **Interviewers**. **Review progress** shows submissions per domain. An interview round has its own tab with an **Interview schedule**: slot length, buffer, day hours, start and end dates, booking, reschedule and cancel notice, and rooms. Turn on **Hold these rooms for interviews** to block other bookings during interview hours. **Schedule** lists every interview and pending invite. From it you can resend an invite, let an applicant rebook, change the location, reassign an interviewer or cancel.

Domain leads run deliberations from their own pages. The delib tab shows each domain's progress.

## Release decisions

1. After a domain closes a delib, its draft decisions become interview invites (on the Interviews step) or final drafts.
2. Interview invites appear under **Interview invites**. Drafts need to be finalized by the domain lead (or by you with **Finalize all** on member cycles).
3. Finalized decisions appear under **Decisions to release**. Release one at a time, or use **Release all** (or **Release shown** when filtered).
4. Confirm. This emails applicants straight away and can't be undone. Accepting someone who still has an open application in another domain also closes that one, and the confirmation names it.
5. When every decision is out, click **Complete cycle** under **Complete the cycle**.

## Waitlists

[Waitlists](/hiring/waitlists) groups waitlisted applicants by domain across cycles. Drag to set the order; if two people share a rank, **Keep this order** settles it. For each person:

- **Accept** promotes them to a member, creates their DALI account and sends the acceptance email, even if the cycle is closed.
- **Remove from waitlist** removes them. On Students cycles they get the continued interest email inviting them to reapply. Use **Remove all from waitlist** for the whole shown list.

## Onboarding

[Onboarding](/hiring/onboarding) lists accepted applicants and their setup progress: **DALI email**, **Slack**, **Figma** (tick the checkbox once invited) and **Profile form**. Filter by start term, cycle and domain, and set a start term from a row. Send a reminder for a step, choosing the channel: **DALI OS**, **Slack DM**, **Email · DALI** or **Email · Dartmouth**.

## Library and Drive folders

[Library](/hiring/library) opens the Hiring folders in Drive: rubrics, application templates and hiring forms. Open a rubric to edit its criteria (label, max score, description) and save a new version. A cycle uses the version you picked for it, so a new version does not change a cycle that is already using an older one. Agreements live here too; open one to see who has signed. [Drive folders](/hiring/drive-folders) sets where new hiring forms and application templates are saved.
