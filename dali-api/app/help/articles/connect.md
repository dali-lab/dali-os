Connect shows where DALI alumni are based, on a map. It is open to current members and alumni.

## Where to find it

[Connect](/connect) is under **Lab** in the sidebar. If your workspace has the Community area, it is the first page there instead. For alumni, Connect is the Home page. See [Alumni](/help/articles/alumni).

## Use the map

- The map has one pin per place. Places come from the **Based in** field on each alum's profile.
- The list beside the map groups alumni by place. Click a place to see only the alumni there, and click the X or **Show all places** to go back.
- Search by name, place or company. Each row shows the class year and current job. Click someone to open their profile.
- A line under the list says how many alumni have no location yet.
- If your own profile has no location, an **Add where you're based** link takes you to your profile to set it.

## Most engaged alumni

The **Most engaged alumni** board ranks alumni by points earned since they graduated. Hover the info icon to see the scoring:

- Taught a workshop or course: 10
- Coffee chat: 5
- Attended a workshop session: 2
- Attended a meeting: 1

Click a name to open the profile.

## Anonymous requests

Resume review requests and coffee chat invites are anonymous until the other person says yes. Members can send both.

### Send a coffee chat invite

1. Open the profile of someone who has coffee chats turned on. A **Coffee chat** button shows in the header only for those people, and never on your own profile.
2. Click it and read the prompt. The person gets a notification and an email, and sees your name only if they accept.
3. Click **Send invite**.

The button then reads **Coffee chat invite sent** and stays that way. The same waiting and declined rules as resume reviews apply. An accepted coffee chat earns points on the leaderboard.

### Send a resume review request

1. Open the profile of someone who has resume reviews turned on. A **Resume review** button shows in the header only for those people.
2. Click it and read the prompt. The person gets a notification and an email, and sees your name only if they accept.
3. Click **Send request**.

The button then reads **Resume review requested** and stays that way. You can't send another to the same person while one is waiting. If they decline, you can't ask again for 30 days, and you are never told they declined, so it looks like a request that is still waiting.

### Answer a request

A notification links to the request page, titled **Coffee chat** or **Resume review**. You see "Someone wants you to review their resume" (or "Someone wants to grab coffee with you" for a coffee chat). Click **Accept** or **Decline**.

- If you accept, the sender is told and you both see each other's name. You get **View profile** and an **Email** link to arrange a time.
- If you decline, the sender isn't told.
- A request can only be answered once.

### Turn requests on or off

Alumni set this on their Home page: open the **Profile** tab and use the **Coffee chats** and **Resume reviews** switches under **I'm open to**. Both are off until you turn them on. When a switch is on, other members and alumni see the matching **Coffee chat** or **Resume review** button on your profile.
