Hiring is where lab members read applications, score them, interview applicants and follow the group decisions. You see the **Hiring** area in the sidebar when you are a reviewer or interviewer on a cycle that is live right now, or when you are Core, an admin or a domain lead. Once your cycle closes, the area goes away for regular members. If you have no hiring role at all, the Hiring pages send you back to the home page.

Applicants use the applicant portal and can't see anything described here.

## Sign the confidentiality agreement

Some cycles require a confidentiality agreement. Until you sign it, application content and your interviews are hidden and you see a prompt to sign. Sign it from that prompt and the content opens up. You can't mark an interview complete until you have signed.

## Find your work

Open [My work](/hiring). It has up to three views, shown only when they apply to you:

- **Reviews**: a board with **Pending**, **In Progress** and **Submitted** columns. Each card shows the applicant, their domain and a button: **Start review**, **Continue review** or **View review**.
- **Interviews**: your scheduled interviews, with upcoming ones first and a **Past interviews** list below.
- **Delibs**: a live, read-only view of your domain's deliberations. It appears when your domain lead has opened a session.

If you review or interview on more than one cycle, cards name the cycle. If nothing is assigned to you yet, the page says so. Reviews are assigned by your domain lead, so you will see applications here after they add you as a reviewer and assign you.

## Review an application

1. On **My work**, open **Reviews** and click **Start review** on a card.
2. Read the application on the left. It includes the general application, the domain challenge if there is one, and collapsed panels for education history, hiring events the applicant attended, prior applications and email history.
3. In **Your review** on the right, drag each slider under **Scoring** to a score from 0 up to that criterion's maximum. The criteria come from the cycle's rubric. Open **Scoring guide** to read what each criterion means. If no rubric is attached yet, the page says "No rubric attached to this application."
4. Write **Internal feedback**. Other reviewers can read it; the applicant never does. Optionally add a **Rejection rationale**: what feedback to give if the group rejects this applicant.
5. Pick an **Overall recommendation**: Strong Hire, Hire, Lean Hire, Lean No Hire or No Hire. Delibs use this to compare reviewers.
6. Click **Submit review**.

Your work saves as you go. The feedback editors only open after the review has been saved once, and until then they show "Save the review first". To change a submitted review, click **Unsubmit and edit** on the review page. You can also drag a card out of the **Submitted** column on the board to reopen it.

## Blind review

A cycle can be set to blind review. While it is on, applicants appear as "Applicant 1", "Applicant 2" and so on instead of by name, and some identity details are hidden. Blinding is per domain. A domain's applicants are named again once that domain sends its interview invites or closes its first deliberation round. An applicant who applied to two domains can be named in one and blind in the other. Core turns blind review on or off for a cycle.

## Browse applications

[Applications](/hiring/applications) lists every application you can see, one row per applicant and domain. Reviewers and domain leads only see the domains they review. Core and admins see every domain, except that Lab members cycles are open only to admins and the reviewers assigned to them. Pick a cycle at the top, search by name or email, and use **Filters** for status, stage, engagement and domain. Click a row to open a read-only page with the answers, scores, interviews and decisions. Reviewers see released decisions only; Core and domain leads also see drafts.

## Interviews

Interviews are booked by applicants from open time on interviewers' DALI OS calendars. If you see a notice to check your calendar, open **Calendar settings** and make sure your availability is current.

- On **My work**, **Interviews**, click **Open interview** on a card.
- The interview page shows the applicant, the reviews on file, the room or a **Join Google Meet** link, your co-interviewer, and any **Interview prep note** the domain leads left during the first delibs.
- You can edit each review's **Rejection rationale** from the **Reviews** card. Changes save as you type and the reviewer sees them too.
- Take notes in **Interview notes**. They are shared with your co-interviewer as you type. You can record and enhance notes with the recorder if it is shown.
- When you finish, choose a **Joint recommendation** and click **Mark complete**. It is one recommendation for both interviewers, and either of you can complete it. Only Core sees **Reopen** on a completed interview; ask Core if it needs changing.
- If you can't make a slot, click **Mark unavailable** on the card. The system looks for a replacement. If none is free, you are asked to contact the hiring lead.

In-person interviews happen in rooms. A cycle can hold its interview rooms so nobody else books them during interview hours; those holds show up as bookings in [Rooms](/rooms).

## Deliberations

After review, domain leads run deliberations: boards where applications are dragged into columns such as No Decision, Interview, Advance, Accept, Waitlist or Reject. You can't move cards, but the **Delibs** view on **My work** mirrors the board live. Click a card to open the applicant's context, including all reviews on that application. Decisions are drafts until a domain lead finalizes them and the hiring lead releases them.
