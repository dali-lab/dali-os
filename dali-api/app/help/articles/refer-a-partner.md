If you know a person or organization that could fund a project with DALI, you can send their details to Core. Core follows up with them, and a referral pays out when the partner signs on. Open [Refer a partner](/refer) under **Lab** in the sidebar. Members and alumni can both use it.

## The first time

The first time you open the page you answer a five question quiz about good referral practice, then click **Submit answers**. Three questions ask which tier a scenario is, one asks what to do before cold-emailing someone for DALI, and one asks when the bounty is paid. You need every answer right to continue. If you miss one, your other answers are kept and the ones to fix are marked, with a note on why. You only take the quiz once.

## Pick a tier

After the quiz, the page shows three tiers with their payouts. Click one to open its form:

- **Cold referral** ($100): you know of a person or organization that could be a partner. You pass the contact on and Core takes it from there.
- **Warm referral** ($250): they are ready to apply to DALI themselves. You give Core their details and tell them to apply at `/partner/apply`.
- **Full pitch** ($500): you pitched a specific project idea and they are on board to fund it through DALI. Core cross-checks that they applied.

Amounts are paid when the partner signs on with DALI. The page also reminds you to be kind and clear when you reach out, and to check with Partner Relations if you aren't sure whether to reach out.

## Send the referral

Fill in the form for your tier and click **Submit referral**. **Pick a different tier** takes you back.

- **Partner name** is always required.
- **Partner email** is optional for a cold referral and required for the other two.
- **Context** is required: why this partner, and what kind of project you imagine.
- **Organization** and **Affiliation** (how you know them) are optional.
- A full pitch also needs **Your pitch**.

You land on a **Referral in** page. Partner Relations is notified, and Core sees your referral in the partner area and marks it when someone has reached out. Questions about a specific referral go to Core.
