DALI OS is where the lab runs its work: projects and tasks, the calendar, documents and files, people, education, hiring and more. You sign in once and move between all of it from one sidebar.

## Home

Click the **dali.os** wordmark at the top of the sidebar to go [Home](/). Home shows a greeting, the week of the term, a search box, and a row of your open tasks (up to six). Click a task card to open it. The rest of your tasks are on [My Tasks](/notifications).

The search box on Home searches people, projects, documents and more as you type. See [Search and commands](/help/articles/search).

## The sidebar

The sidebar has the same pieces on every page, top to bottom:

- **Bell**: your tasks and unread notifications. The red number is how many need you. See [Notifications](/help/articles/notifications).
- **Hide sidebar** button: collapses the sidebar. Press ⌘\ to toggle it. To bring it back, use the **Show sidebar** button or press ⌘\ again. On a phone, the menu button at the top opens the sidebar, and the bell opens [My Tasks](/notifications).
- **Search**: opens the command palette (⌘K).
- **Calendar**, **Email** and **Drive**: always one click away. Alumni do not see Email or Drive.
- **Favorites**: pages you starred. Click the star in the page bar (or press ⌘D) to add or remove the current page.
- **Section switcher and its pages**: pick a section, then pick a page inside it.
- **Upcoming**: your next meetings. Click one to open it.
- **Account menu** at the bottom: **Profile**, **Settings**, **Help**, and **Log out**. In a browser on a computer it also has **Get the desktop app**.

## Sections

The section switcher shows only the sections your roles allow.

- **Lab**: **Projects**, **People**, **Groups** (Core, admins and instructors), **Mentorship** (mentors and Core), **Attendance**, and **Refer a partner**.
- **Education**: **Hub**, **Offerings**, and **CE Compliance** (Core only).
- **Core**: staffing, partners, payroll and the other lab processes. Only Core sees it.
- **Hiring**: applications and reviews. Core, admins and domain leads always see it. Other members see it only while they review or interview on a cycle that is open right now.
- **Admin**: members, roles and system settings. Only Core sees it.

Alumni get a smaller sidebar with one section.

## The page bar

The bar across the top of each page shows where you are.

- **Back and forward arrows** move through pages you have visited. They appear in the desktop app only. A browser has its own.
- The section icon and breadcrumb show the section and page. Click a crumb to open it.
- Page-specific buttons, such as presence avatars, appear on the right.
- **Open this page's guide** appears on pages that have a short guide. Click it to read the guide in place of the page, and close it to go back.
- The star adds the page to your favorites.
- **More actions** (the three dots) holds **Copy link**. Press ⌘⇧C to copy the link without opening the menu. In a browser, it also holds **Open in desktop app**.

## Open a document beside the page

You can read or edit a document without leaving the page you are on. Hold Option (Alt on Windows and Linux) and click any link to a document. You can also select a search result with ⌘Enter. The document opens in a panel on the right.

- Drag the panel's left edge to resize it.
- **Open full page** turns the panel into the normal page.
- **Close panel** (or Esc) closes it.
- On a wide screen the panel stays open as you move to other pages.
- On a narrow screen the panel covers the page, and Back closes it.

Only documents open this way. Other pages open as normal pages.

## Find your way around

- Press ⌘K from anywhere to jump to a page, person or document.
- **Help** in the account menu opens [Help](/help). The list of [keyboard shortcuts](/help/shortcuts) is there too.
- Use ⌘K and run **Start the tour** to replay the intro tour.
- Use ⌘K and run **Hide sidebar (focus mode)** to work without the sidebar. The page reloads.

On Windows and Linux, use Ctrl where this article says ⌘, and Alt where it says Option.
