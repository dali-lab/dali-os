Paid work at DALI is logged on the Timesheet view of the [Calendar](/calendar). Each entry is a block of time with the role you worked it under and a short note. Hours are tracked by pay period, and you submit them through JobX.

## Switch to the Timesheet view

Open [Calendar](/calendar) and click **Timesheet** in the toggle at the top (the other choice is **Calendar**). The grid then shows your logged hours instead of events. The left rail changes to your roles and hour totals.

## Log hours

There are two ways to add an entry:

- Click **Log hours** at the top right, on the Week or Day view. A **New timesheet entry** window opens.
- In the week or day grid, drag across the time you worked. A **New timesheet entry** card opens for that slot, and you can keep resizing the block on the grid while the card is open.

Fill in:

- **Starts** and **Ends**. The end must be after the start, and one entry cannot be longer than 24 hours. The hours are worked out for you.
- **Role**: the paid role the hours belong to. This is required.
- **Note**: what you worked on. This is required.

Click **Add entry**. The block appears on the grid right away.

## Edit or delete an entry

Click an entry on the grid to open **Edit timesheet entry**. Change the times, role or note and save (a role and a note are both required), or choose **Delete** and confirm. Deleting takes the hours off your timesheet for that pay period. You can only change your own entries, and every entry must keep a role.

## Hours from meetings

- Open a meeting you are invited to on the calendar and switch on **Add to timesheet** in its details to log that meeting's time on your own timesheet. It starts with no role. Switch it off to remove it.
- When someone marks you present at a meeting that tracks attendance, or you check yourself in, an entry for that meeting is added for you. These entries start with no role. Open one and pick a role and a note, since an entry cannot be saved without them.
- When you create an event, the **Add to timesheet** option on the form logs it against the role you pick, with a note.

## Your roles

The **Roles** list in the left rail is what you can log hours against. It holds:

- Your project roles this term, named like "Project name Domain" (with "Mentor" added for P3 mentors).
- Core roles, instructor roles for education offerings, and domain lead roles.
- Any outside job you added yourself.

Admin access is not a paid role, so it does not appear. If you have no paid roles this term, the form tells you there is nothing to log hours against.

To add a job outside DALI, click **Add role** at the bottom of the rail, type a name like "Baker Library front desk" and click **Add**. Remove it with the **x** on its chip or the remove control on its row. Hours already logged against a removed job stay on your timesheet. Only jobs you added yourself can be removed. Project and Core roles come from staffing.

Click a role's colour swatch to choose its colour on the grid.

## Weekly and pay period totals

Above the role list, choose **Week** or **Pay period** to set what the hour totals cover. The dates are shown underneath and the choice is remembered. Each role shows its hours, and **Total** adds them up.

A pay period is two weeks, Sunday through Saturday, the first starting on July 5, 2026 and repeating every 14 days. Period boundaries are marked on the grid.

## Reminders

DALI OS sends a **Timesheet reminders** notification about a day before each pay period closes, to members who hold a paid role. In-app, desktop and Slack DM are on by default, and email is off. Change this in your notification settings. See [Notifications and tasks](/help/articles/notifications).

## Submit in JobX

DALI OS is where you log hours, and nothing here sends them to payroll. You submit your timesheet in Dartmouth's JobX. A Chrome extension adds a **Fill from DALI** button on the JobX timesheet page. You choose a role and a pay period, review the entries, and it fills JobX one day at a time. It never submits for you, so you still submit the period in JobX yourself. Entries that cross midnight are split by day.
