Your profile is what other members see about you. Settings is where you control how DALI OS looks, what it sends you, and which accounts and devices are connected.

Open either from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar: click your name, then **Profile** or **Settings**.

## Your profile

[Profile](/profile) opens your own member page, the same page other members see at their own address. It has two tabs: **Profile** and **Activity**. Mentors and Core also see a **Mentorship** tab when they open someone else's profile.

On the **Profile** tab:

- **About**: pronouns, handle, NetID, major, class year, hometown, where you are based, birthday and dietary restrictions.
- **Contact**: email, phone, personal site, time zone, GitHub username and LinkedIn URL.
- **Work experience**: past jobs. Use **Add** to add one, and the edit and remove buttons on a job to change it.
- **Roles, domains & levels**, and **Groups** (Core, admins and instructors see groups).

To change About or Contact, click **Edit** on the card, make your changes, and click **Save**. **Cancel** drops them. To change your photo, click the camera button on it and choose a file. Roles, domains and levels are set by Core, so you cannot edit them yourself.

The **Activity** tab shows your project history, reviews waiting on you, education history, and compliance items. When you view someone else's profile, you only see what your role allows.

Admins can edit any member's About and Contact cards. Everyone else can edit only their own.

## Settings tabs

[Settings](/settings) has four tabs.

### General

[General](/settings/general) holds the settings that shape your workspace:

- **Appearance**: choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Match system**. This applies to the device you are on.
- **Workspace**: in the desktop app, choose **Tabs** or **No tabs** (the page reloads). In a browser, you can switch on **Open links in the desktop app**. **Emoji suggestions** lets you type : in a doc to pick an emoji. Both are saved on the device you change them on.
- **Presence**: turn on **Appear away** to hide your online dot from other members. You still get notifications.
- **Membership pass**: add your DALI pass to **Apple Wallet** or **Google Wallet** to check in at events. Resetting the pass stops every copy working, so you would add the new one again.

### Notifications

[Notifications](/settings/notifications) is where you choose, per kind of notification, whether it shows in-app, as a desktop banner, as a Slack DM, or by email. See [Notifications and tasks](/help/articles/notifications).

### Connections

[Connections](/settings/connections) lists the accounts DALI OS is linked to:

- **Google Calendar**: click **Google account** to add one. Its calendars then show in Calendar. Each linked account shows **Syncing** or **Not syncing**. Click **Disconnect** to remove it. You choose which calendars are visible from Calendar itself.
- **Slack**: click **Connect Slack**. DALI OS finds your Slack account by the emails on your profile. Once connected, you can get Slack DMs and be added to project channels when you are staffed. **Disconnect** stops both and keeps your notification choices.
- **AI & connected apps**: apps you have let read or act on DALI OS for you, such as Claude over MCP. Click **Revoke** to cut an app off right away. You can authorize it again later. The same apps also appear in **Where you're signed in** below. [How to connect Claude](/help/mcp) explains setup.

### Sign-in & security

[Sign-in & security](/settings/security) has two sections:

- **Passkeys**: click **Passkey** to add one, then sign in with Face ID, Touch ID or a security key. Add a passkey on each device you use. You can rename or remove them. Without one, you sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email.
- **Where you're signed in**: your browsers, the desktop app and connected apps. The device you are on is marked **This device**. Click **Sign out** (or **Revoke** for an app) on a row to end that session. **Sign out others** signs out every device but this one, including the desktop app and connected apps, and they must sign in again.

Older links to calendar, Slack, sessions or connected apps settings take you to the matching tab.
