Groups are named sets of members. You pick a group instead of adding people one by one, for example when inviting people to an event, sharing a document or sending an announcement.

Only Core, Admin and Instructors can open the Groups page. Anyone else who goes to it lands on [People](/members).

## Open the page

Go to [Groups](/members/groups) under **Lab** in the sidebar. Groups are listed in sections: **Custom groups**, **Lab-wide**, **Projects**, **Domains**, **Terms** and **Offerings**.

- Use the **Active**, **Archived** and **All** switch to change which groups show.
- Search by group name.
- Turn on **Show empty auto groups** to include automatic groups that have nobody in them. They are hidden by default.

Click a group's name or the arrow to open it and see its roster. Each person links to their profile. Each group card shows its member count, and groups tied to specific terms show the term codes.

## Two kinds of group

**Custom groups** are the ones you make. You choose who is in them and you can change them.

**Auto groups** carry an **Auto** badge. They fill in by themselves from assignments: Core, the hiring team, mentors, alumni, and one group per project, domain, term and offering. You can't edit or delete them. Project and domain groups have an **Open in People directory** button that opens the directory filtered to the same people.

## Create a group

1. Click **New group**.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Under **Duration**, choose **Ongoing** (stays active until you archive it) or **For specific term(s)** and pick the terms. A term-bound group archives itself once those terms have ended.
4. Search for and select members. At least one is required. Only current-term lab members can be picked.
5. Click **Create group**.

## Change a custom group

- **Add member**: open the group, click **Add member**, search, and click a name.
- Remove someone with the remove button on their card, then confirm **Remove**.
- **Archive group** (the archive icon) hides it from the **Active** view. **Restore group** brings it back from **Archived**.
- You can't remove the last person from a group. Delete the group instead.
- **Delete group** (the trash icon) removes it for good. Any document shares and scheduling audiences that use the group stop working, so archive instead if you are unsure.

People who have since become alumni still appear in an existing group's roster, but you can only add current-term members.

## Where groups are used

Groups show up wherever you choose an audience, such as inviting people to a calendar event, choosing who an announcement goes to, and sharing documents. Each person's profile also lists their groups. See [Sharing](/help/articles/sharing) for sharing a document with a group.
