The Community area groups the places where members and alumni share things: Community, Opportunities, Gallery and Stories. It is available when the Community feature is turned on for you.

## Gallery

[Gallery](/community/gallery) is a photo feed from the lab. Members, staff and alumni all see it.

### Post photos

1. Click **Post**.
2. Choose photos or drop them into the window. You can add up to 10 at a time. PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF work.
3. Add an optional caption to each photo, up to 500 characters.
4. Click **Post**, or **Post 3** if you picked three photos. Each photo becomes its own post.

### Browse and search

- **All** and **Mine** switch between everyone's posts and your own.
- The search box finds posts by caption and people by name. Pick a person to see only their posts, and clear the chip to go back.
- The feed loads more as you scroll. A **new posts** button appears at the top when others post.

### Open a post

Click a photo to open it with its caption, author, reactions and comments. The **More** menu has:

- **Copy link**
- **Download**
- **Report** (on other people's posts). Add a reason if you want. After 2 reports a post is hidden until Core reviews it.
- **Delete** (on your own posts). The photo, its reactions and its comments are removed for good.
- **Hide post** and **Restore post**, for Core only.

## Stories

[Stories](/resources) is the lab's front page for member stories. Its masthead reads **Everything DALI**, and the back link on a post returns there. Lab members and Core can read and write. Alumni see the Stories link too.

### Read

Published posts are laid out like a newspaper. Pinned posts lead, then the rest newest first. Click a post to read it. A globe icon marks a post that is also public on the DALI website.

Under a published post you can add emoji reactions and comments. Type `@` in a comment to mention someone. The author is notified of new reactions and comments. You can delete your own comments, and Core can delete any.

### Write a post

1. Click **Write**. A new draft opens, or your last empty draft is reused.
2. Add a title and write in the editor. Press `/` for blocks. It saves as you type.
3. Click the **Post details** icon to add a cover image, a short summary shown under the headline, and the **Public** switch. **Public** also puts the post on the DALI website. **Internal** posts stay in DALI OS.
4. Click **Submit for review**.

Only an Admin publishes. When you submit, Admins are notified and the post shows under **Your drafts** with a review status. You get a notification when it is approved. Click **Withdraw** to pull it back to a draft. After a post is published you can click **Unpublish**.

When an Admin writes a post, the button reads **Publish**. Admins also see **Waiting for approval** on the front page and an **Approve** button on a submitted post.

You can edit or delete your own posts with the pencil and trash icons on the post page. Core can do the same on any post. Deleting a public post also removes it from the website.

### Pin posts to the top (Core)

Core sees an **Edit** button on the front page. Turn it on to **Pin to top**, **Unpin**, or **Move earlier** and **Move later**. Click **Done** when finished. A post must be published before it can be pinned.
