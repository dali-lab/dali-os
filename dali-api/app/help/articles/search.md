The command palette is the fastest way around DALI OS. One box finds people, projects, tasks, documents and help articles, jumps to any page you can open, and runs a few commands.

## Open it

- Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) from any page.
- Or click **Search** at the top of the sidebar. On a phone, use the search button in the top bar.

Type to search. Use the up and down arrows to move through results, and press Enter to open the highlighted one. Press Esc to close the palette.

On [Home](/) there is also a search box on the page. It uses the same search, but the results appear right under the box instead of in the palette.

## What it finds

Type at least two letters to search. Results are grouped under headings:

- **People**: members, by name or email. Click one to open their profile.
- **Projects** and **Tasks**: tasks open on their project's board.
- **Drive**: documents, forms and uploaded project files.
- **Education**: course offerings.
- **Groups**: lab groups. Only Core, admins and instructors get these.
- **Guides** and **Help**: guides and help articles.
- **Applicants**, **Hiring library**, **Hiring cycles**, **Partners** and **Partner applications**: these appear only if your role gives you access to them. Core sees all of them. Domain leads also see rubrics and agreements in the hiring library.

You only see results you are allowed to open. If a person or document is missing, you may not have access to it.

## Go to a page

With nothing typed, the palette lists pages under **Go to**. This is the same list as your sidebar: Home, **My Tasks**, **Calendar**, **Email**, **Drive**, **Room booking**, every section and page you can reach, plus **Profile**, **Settings** and **Help**.

Type part of a page name to narrow the list. For example, "staff" finds the staffing pages. Core members also get **Core** and **Admin** headings that list each tool in those areas, such as payroll or roles.

## Commands

Commands appear under **Commands** and match by name as you type.

- **Hide sidebar (focus mode)** or **Show sidebar**: toggles the sidebar. The page reloads.
- **Start the tour**: replays the intro tour.
- **Copy link to this page**: copies the address of the page you are on.
- **Open this page in the desktop app** and **Download the desktop app**: appear when you use DALI OS in a browser on a computer. See [Desktop app](/help/articles/desktop-app).
- **Log out**.

## Open a result beside your page

Hold ⌘ (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) and press Enter, or hold it while you click. The result opens without taking over the page you are on:

- A document opens in the side panel next to your page.
- In the desktop app with tabs on, other pages open in a new background tab.
- In a browser, other pages open in a new browser tab.

## Tips

- The palette is only for finding and jumping. To change something, open the result and use that page.
