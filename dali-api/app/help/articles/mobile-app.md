The mobile app is a companion to DALI OS for iPhone and Android. It has five tabs along the bottom: **Time**, **Project**, **Calendar**, **Notes** and **Pass**. It uses the same data as the website, so what you do on your phone shows up on the web, and the other way around.

The app is built for lab members.

## Sign in

Open the app and start sign-in. The app opens DALI OS in your browser and shows a code. Sign in there, approve the device, and the app signs you in. You stay signed in on that phone until you tap **Sign out** in your profile sheet.

## Time

The **Time** tab is your timesheet.

- **Clock in** starts a timer. Type what you are working on, and choose the role to bill the time to with **Bill time to**. **Clock out** logs one entry. The timer keeps running if you close the app.
- **Add time** logs hours you forgot to clock, with a start and end time.
- **Entries** lists what you logged in the current pay period. Tap one to edit it. Swipe an entry or use **Delete** to remove it.
- A meeting you added to your timesheet appears as an entry. Use **Remove** to take it off.

These are the same entries as the timesheet on the web Calendar. You need a role this term to log hours.

## Project

The **Project** tab shows the project you are staffed on this term, with the sprint's tasks. Tap a task to see its status, assignees, dates, linked items and activity. You can change a task's status, and the change shows on the DALI OS board. **Your other tasks** lists tasks you have on other projects.

## Calendar

The **Calendar** tab shows your week. **Up next** shows your next meeting.

- Tap a meeting to see its details. If you were invited, answer with **Going**, **Maybe** or **Can't make it**.
- **Add to timesheet** puts a meeting on your timesheet.
- **New event** creates a DALI OS meeting you organize. Give it a title, start time and length, search for people to invite, and optionally add a location or a Google Meet link. Turn on **Add to Google Calendar** to put it on your linked calendar with email invites.

## Notes

The **Notes** tab records meeting notes.

- Open a meeting and tap **Record meeting notes**, or tap **Record without a meeting** for a quick recording. If the tab says recording isn't on for you yet, you can still read your notes. Choose where the notes go: **My notes** or a notebook you can edit.
- While recording you can pause, resume, minimize the recorder, or discard the recording.
- When you finish, the app transcribes the audio and writes notes into the page. The recording shows **Processing** until it is done. If transcription fails, it shows **Couldn't process**.
- **Recent notes** lists your notes. Tap one to open it in the DALI OS editor and edit it.
- Use **Take notes** on a meeting to open that meeting's notes page and write without recording.

Recording needs your microphone.

## Pass

The **Pass** tab shows your **Member pass** with a QR code. Tap it to show the code full screen, and turn up your brightness when scanning at the door. **Recent check-ins** lists where you were checked in lately.

## Your profile

Tap your photo at the top right of any tab to open your profile sheet. It shows your name, roles, groups, handle, NetID, email and where you are based. Set your status to active or away, or sign out. To change details, use the website.
