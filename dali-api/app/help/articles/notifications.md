DALI OS gathers everything that needs you in one place: the bell at the top of the sidebar. You choose, per kind of notification, whether it also reaches you by email, Slack or a desktop banner.

## The bell

The number on the bell is how many things need your attention. Click it to open the **Tasks** panel. It lists:

- things you have to act on, such as meeting invites, forms to fill and onboarding steps;
- unread notifications, such as comments, assignments and announcements;
- project tasks assigned to you.

Items you have to act on stay until you deal with them. Other notifications clear once you dismiss or open them. Click **See all** in the panel to open [My Tasks](/notifications).

## My Tasks

[My Tasks](/notifications) is the full list. It has three tabs:

- **Project work**: tasks assigned to you on projects.
- **Meetings & events**: invites and other things waiting on you.
- **History**: everything you were sent. Filter it by **All**, **Open** and **Cleared**.

Each row has the buttons to deal with it right there. A project task has **Mark done**. A form reminder opens the form, and you can dismiss it. A plain notification has **Dismiss**.

## Reply to a meeting invite

When someone schedules a meeting with you, an invite appears in the bell and on My Tasks with three buttons: **Going**, **Maybe** and **Can't make it**. Pick one and the invite clears. Desktop banners use the same three buttons. If your Google Calendar is connected, your answer is also sent to the event there.

## Reminders

- **Meeting reminders** arrive shortly before a meeting you are in starts, 15 minutes by default.
- **Task deadline reminders** arrive a day before a task you are assigned is due, and again at the end of its due day.
- Reminders for things that no longer apply, such as a cancelled meeting, go away on their own.

## Old meeting notifications clear on their own

You don't have to dismiss meeting notifications that are out of date. They leave the bell, the **Open** filter in History and desktop banners by themselves:

- a meeting reminder, once the meeting starts;
- an invite you never answered, once a one-off meeting has happened (invites to a repeating meeting stay, since later meetings can still be answered);
- **Record this meeting?**, 3 hours after it arrives;
- a cancellation notice, after 3 days;
- a proposed new time, after 7 days.

They are still listed under **All** in History, marked **Expired**.

## Choose what reaches you

Open [Settings → Notifications](/settings/notifications). Each kind of notification is a row, grouped by area such as Meetings, Tasks, Documents and Announcements. Each row has four choices:

- **In-app**: shows in the bell and My Tasks.
- **Desktop**: a native banner from the [desktop app](/help/articles/desktop-app). It only applies to things that are also in-app.
- **Slack DM**: a direct message from DALI OS.
- **Email**: **Instantly**, in a **Daily digest**, in a **Weekly digest**, or off.

Change as many rows as you like, then click **Save**. A bar shows **Unsaved changes** until you save, and **Discard** throws them away.

Some details:

- **Locked rows**: things you have to act on, like meeting invites, always show in-app. Their in-app box is switched on and cannot be turned off.
- **Digests**: a daily or weekly email lists what you have not read, so choosing a digest keeps in-app on for that row. Use **Digests arrive at** at the top of the page to pick the time: 8 AM, noon, 6 PM or the lab default (Eastern Time). Weekly digests go out on the day shown on that page.
- **Sent separately**: a few emails, such as education application decisions, come from their own process. They are sent whatever you choose here.
- **Core and admin rows**: some rows, such as partner updates, only appear for Core or admins.
- If you have never changed a row, it uses the lab's default for that kind of notification.

## Students and partners

Dartmouth students in the portal and partners get a simpler version. Their bell opens one **Notifications** list, with no project work or meetings tabs. They choose what reaches them in a **Notifications** section on their own settings page, [portal settings](/portal/settings) or [partner settings](/partner/settings), with just **In-app** and **Email** (**Instantly** or off).

- Students see their course notifications: announcements, assignments, discussion replies, feedback requests, certificates, session reminders and grades.
- Partners see replies to their comments on shared project documents. The link opens the document in the partner portal.
- Since they rarely open DALI OS, new assignments, grades, session reminders, feedback requests and comment replies email them by default.

## Slack DMs

Slack DM boxes stay switched off until you connect Slack. Go to [Settings → Connections](/settings/connections), find **Slack**, and click **Connect Slack**. DALI OS looks you up by the emails on your account, so your Slack account must use one of them. See [Profile and settings](/help/articles/profile-and-settings).

## Desktop banners

In the [desktop app](/help/articles/desktop-app), new in-app items also show as native banners when the **Desktop** box is checked for that row. Meeting invites on a banner have **Going**, **Maybe** and **Can't make it** buttons. Other banners have **Mark read**. Time-sensitive items, such as a meeting about to start, play a sound.
