The desktop app is DALI OS in its own window. It is the same site you use in a browser, with a few things a browser cannot do: tabs, native notification banners, and a panel in your menu bar or tray.

## Download and install

Open [Get the desktop app](/download). You can reach it from the account menu in the sidebar, or from the **Download** button in the banner that appears in a browser on a computer. The page picks your system for you:

- **macOS 12 or later**: open the downloaded `.dmg`, drag DALI OS to your Applications folder, then open it from there. One download works for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
- **Windows 10 or later**: run the downloaded `.exe` and follow the setup. If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux**: make the downloaded `.AppImage` executable, then run it. You need Secret Service support (GNOME Keyring or KWallet).

The first time you open the app, click **Sign in**. Your browser opens to an approval page. Check that the code there matches the one in the app, then click **Approve device**. The app signs in on its own. If the browser did not open, click **Open it again** in the app.

The app checks for updates at launch and once a day, downloads them in the background, and tells you to reopen it to finish. To update right away, choose **Check for Updates…** in the **DALI OS** menu. It installs the update and restarts the app.

## Tabs

The app opens pages in tabs above the page, so you can keep several open. In a browser, you get one page at a time.

- Click the **New tab** button, or press ⌘T (Ctrl+T on Windows and Linux).
- Press ⌘W to close a tab. ⌘⇧T reopens the last one you closed.
- Press ⌘⇧] and ⌘⇧[ to move to the next and previous tab. ⌘1 to ⌘8 jump to that tab, and ⌘9 jumps to the last one.
- Right-click a tab for **Pin tab**, **Copy link**, **Close tab** and **Close other tabs**. Pinned tabs stay at the left.
- Press ⌘Enter on a search result to open it in a background tab.

To turn tabs off, go to [Settings → General](/settings/general), find **Workspace**, and choose **No tabs**. Choose **Tabs** to turn them back on. See [keyboard shortcuts](/help/shortcuts) for the full list, including back, forward, find and zoom.

## Notification banners

The app shows new notifications as banners from your computer, even when the window is in the background. Click a banner to open its page.

- Meeting invites on a banner have **Accept**, **Maybe** and **Decline** buttons, so you can reply without opening the app.
- Other banners have **Mark read**.
- Time-sensitive items, such as a meeting about to start, play a sound.

macOS asks for permission the first time. If you said no, turn banners back on for DALI OS in System Settings.

To choose which notifications become banners, open [Settings → Notifications](/settings/notifications) and use the **Desktop** column. See [Notifications and tasks](/help/articles/notifications).

## Menu bar and tray panel

The app puts an icon in your menu bar (macOS) or system tray, with your unread count on it. On macOS and Windows, click it to open a small panel with:

- your next meeting, with **Join**, **Record** and **Open notes**, and **Stop** while a recording is running;
- the rest of today;
- **Open DALI OS**, a bell for your notifications, and a gear for notification settings.

Right-click the icon for a short menu with **Open DALI OS**, the version and **Quit**. On Linux, the icon shows this menu directly and adds your unread items to it.

## Open pages in the app from your browser

In a browser on a computer, the page bar's **More actions** menu has **Open in desktop app**. The same command is in the ⌘K palette. In [Settings → General](/settings/general), **Open links in the desktop app** sends links you open in that browser to the app. It is saved per browser.

## Good to know

- The app always loads the live DALI OS site, so it needs an internet connection.
- **Open at Login** in the **DALI OS** menu starts the app when you sign in to your computer.
- **Sign out others** in [Settings → Sign-in & security](/settings/security) also signs out the desktop app.
