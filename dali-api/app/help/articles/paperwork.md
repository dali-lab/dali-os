[Paperwork](/paperwork), under **Lab** in the sidebar, collects the lab tasks that come up now and then. The number beside it counts what is waiting on you. Alumni do not see it.

## Waiting on you

Expenses sent back to you and documents that need your signature are listed first, each with a button that opens it. When nothing is waiting, the page says so instead.

## This term

The staffing forms Core has opened for the round, such as intent to work and project bids, show here with whether you have sent each one. Your classes for the term show too, with a link that opens the classes settings in Calendar. Any Core or fellowship application you can apply to right now is listed with its due date. Each row shows **To do**, **Open** or **Done**.

When Core hasn't opened anything for the term, the section says nothing is open yet.

## Anytime

Tiles for things you can do at any point:

- **File an expense** opens [Expenses](/expenses), and notes how many approved expenses are not paid back yet.
- **Staff cards you can use** lists the cards you can file on. It only shows if you can use one.
- **Refer a partner** opens [Refer a partner](/refer).
- **Send feedback** opens the DALI OS feedback page.
- **Your certificates** and **Signed documents** show once you have at least one.

## Set up once

A short checklist: adding your lab pass to your phone's wallet, finishing your onboarding profile, and installing the desktop app. Seniors also see an email for after graduation, where the alumni invite is sent. The section disappears once everything on it is done.

Expenses and Refer a partner are also in ⌘K: search for **New expense** or **Refer a partner**.
