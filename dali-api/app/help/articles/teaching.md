Instructors and Core manage an offering from its **Manage** page. Core can manage every offering. An instructor manages the offerings they are assigned to. Students see the result in their course hub. See [Education](/help/articles/education) for the student side.

## Open the Manage page

Go to [Offerings](/education/offerings) and open your offering, then click **Manage**. From the course hub you can also click **Manage**, and **Student view** shows the hub exactly as a student sees it. Managers also see their own draft and archived offerings in the Offerings list.

The Manage page has tabs: **Details**, **Sessions**, **Applications**, **Roster**, **Materials**, **Assignments**, **Discussion** and **Feedback**.

## Create an offering (Core)

Only Core can create one. On Offerings, click **New offering**, fill in the title, type, capacity, registration window and, for miniseries and fellowships, the **Certificate requirement**. Click **Create draft**. The checkbox **Applications need instructor review** is on by default for miniseries and fellowships. Turn it off for RSVP, where people are approved automatically up to capacity.

A new offering is a draft and nobody sees it. Add sessions, then click **Publish** at the top of the Manage page. Publishing needs at least one session and a valid registration window. **Unpublish** takes it back to a draft. Core can delete a draft with **Delete draft**.

## Details

On the **Details** tab you can edit the course details and description (**Save details**), set the feedback forms, and edit the application form with **Edit form**. Core also assigns instructors (**Save instructors**), invites an external instructor by name and NetID (**Invite**), views decision emails, and picks the completion certificate design. The offering type is locked after it is created.

## Sessions

On the **Sessions** tab, click **Add session** for one at a time, or **Generate weekly series** to create a run of sessions on chosen weekdays. Each session has a topic, start and end, location, recording URL and notes for students. Deleting a session also deletes its attendance.

## Materials

On the **Materials** tab you can add:

- a read-only page (**Add material**) that students open from the course,
- a folder (**Add folder**) to group them,
- uploaded files (**Upload files**),
- a shared doc (**Add shared doc**) that enrolled students co-edit live.

Attach any of them to a session or leave them on the whole offering.

## Applications

On the **Applications** tab, filter by status and open an application to read the answers. Click **Approve**, **Waitlist** or **Reject**. **Approve all N pending** approves applications in the order they were submitted until capacity is reached. Anyone beyond that stays pending, so decide them yourself. When an approved student withdraws, the waitlist moves up. You can reorder the waitlist. Applicants get a notification when their status changes, and an email if one is set up for that decision.

## Attendance

Open the **Roster** tab. Click **Take attendance**, mark each student **Present**, **Absent** or **Excused**, then **Done**. A Present mark also earns the student a CE credit.

To let students mark themselves, pick the session and click **Open self check-in**. A QR code and link appear to project in class. Click **Close check-in** when you are done. It also closes on its own about 30 minutes after the session ends. The roster also has a **Performance** view of grades per assignment.

## Assignments and grading

On the **Assignments** tab, click **Add assignment** and set a title, due date (optional), type, session (optional) and points (optional). Types are Text, File, Text + files, Link / URL, Document (in-app) and Mark complete only.

Open an assignment to see each student's submission. Enter a score if the assignment has points, add a grade (for example "Complete") and feedback, then click **Release grade**. Click **Update grade** to change it later.

## Announcements and discussion

The **Discussion** tab has **New post** for the class discussion and **New announcement**, which notifies every approved student in the app and by email, depending on their notification settings. Students can also post and reply here.

## Feedback

The **Feedback** tab shows responses to the session feedback form, which is sent to students marked Present after each session. Instructors see responses anonymized and shuffled, and only once there are at least three. Core sees names and the instructor exit survey.

## Certificate templates (Core)

Core designs completion certificates in [Certificate templates](/education/certificate-templates). Open a template to place fields on its background image.

- **Add field** puts a text box on the page. Drag it to move it, and drag a side handle to set how wide it is. Shift-click or drag across the page to select several fields, then use **Align** to line them up with the page or with each other.
- Arrow keys nudge the selected fields, **Delete** removes them, and ⌘Z undoes the last change. Guides appear as you drag. Hold ⌘ to skip snapping.
- In the bar above a selected field, choose its font size, bold, alignment and color. **Wrap** breaks long text onto more lines inside the box. **Shrink** makes the text smaller until it fits on one line. The student name shrinks by default.
- Changes save on their own. Saving a template restyles every certificate already issued from it, including ones students have already downloaded.

## Close out

When the course is done, click **Close out course** at the top. Everyone approved who met the attendance requirement gets a certificate and a notification, and the course is archived. Excused absences count toward the requirement. Fix attendance first, because you can re-run close-out later and it only issues missing certificates. If the course has not finished, the confirmation warns you before you continue. **Reopen** unarchives it and keeps certificates already issued.
