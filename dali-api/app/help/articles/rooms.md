[Rooms](/rooms) shows every bookable DALI room side by side for one day, so you can see what is free and book it. Any lab member can book. You can also reach it from the door icon (**Room booking**) at the top of [Calendar](/calendar).

## Book a room

1. Open [Rooms](/rooms). Use the arrows, the date field or **Today** to pick the day.
2. Click or drag on an empty spot in a room's column, or click **Book** for the next free slot.
3. In **Book a room**, check the **Room**, **Date**, **Starts** and **Ends**. Add **What for** if you want a label.
4. Click **Book**.

Rules the booking has to meet:

- It can be at most 8 hours long.
- It can't start in the past, and can't be more than 90 days ahead.
- It can't overlap anything already in the room. If it does, the message names what holds the room.

For a repeating booking, set **Repeat** and give it an end. A repeating booking is checked for every date, and it can have up to 200 dates.

Each room column shows how many it seats and where it is, when an admin has filled that in. Meetings that hold a room show on the schedule too.

## Change or cancel a booking

Click a booking to see who booked it and when. The buttons you see depend on who you are:

- You can **Edit** and **Cancel** your own bookings that have not ended. Core can do this for anyone's.
- A booking that is already underway can't move its start or change its room. Its button reads **End** instead of **Cancel**, and ending frees the rest of the slot.
- On a repeating booking you choose **This time only** or **Whole series** to edit, and **This time only**, **This and following** or **Whole series** to cancel. A whole series can change its time, name or room, but not its days.
- Bookings for interviews show **Managed by hiring** and are changed from the hiring cycle page.
- Meetings on the schedule are read-only here. Click **Open in Calendar** to change them.

## Rooms held by calendar events

When you create an event or meeting in Calendar, the **Location** field suggests DALI rooms. Pick one and the room is held for that event. A room that something else already holds is marked unavailable. For a repeating event the check covers every date.

- Typing any other location in the field drops the hold, so what the field says is what gets booked.
- All-day events can't hold a room.
- If you change an event's time, the room hold moves with it. If you delete the event, the room is released.
- A hold from an event can't be edited on the Rooms page. It says **Booked from a calendar event**. Change it from the event. Core can cancel one from Rooms to free the room, and the event stays.

## Door displays

A door display is an iPad outside the rooms. Each iPad shows two rooms, and each room reads as available, starting soon or in use, with what is on now and today's schedule.

People can walk up and scan their DALI wallet pass to:

- **Book now** for 15, 30 or 60 minutes, as long as the length ends before the next booking, or choose **Custom** to drag out any time, up to 90 days ahead. While the room is in use, the button is **Book later**.
- **End early** or **Extend 15 min** on a room booking underway. Only the person who booked it, or Core, can do this. Extending only works if it doesn't run into the next booking. Meetings and interview bookings can't be changed at the door.
- Check in to an event. From 15 minutes before a self check-in event starts until 15 minutes after it ends, that room's panel becomes a check-in station. When someone turns on iPad scanning from a meeting's page, the whole screen becomes a scanner. See [Attendance](/help/articles/attendance).

### Set up a display (Core)

Core manages rooms and displays at [Admin, Rooms](/admin/rooms).

1. Click **Add room** to create a room with a **Name**, **Where it is** and **Seats**. Use the pencil to edit a room and the archive button to retire one. Archived rooms can't be booked.
2. Under a room's **Door displays**, add a display. Give it a **Display name**, pick the **Other room** it also shows, and click **Get setup code**.
3. On the iPad, open the DALI OS app and enter the code. It works for 10 minutes. The page shows **Waiting for setup** until the iPad connects.
4. To remove a display, click **Remove display**. The iPad stops working right away.

To unpair from the iPad itself, press and hold the date in the top right for 3 seconds.

If iPad scanning is on for an event, a banner on the page says **Scanning on iPads**. **Stop scanning** returns every display to showing its room.
