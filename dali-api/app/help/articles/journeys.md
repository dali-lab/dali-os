Journeys are self-paced onboarding courses, grouped by domain. Each journey is a set of modules with lessons, slides, an optional lab and an optional quiz. Your progress saves as you go.

## Find journeys

Open [Journeys](/journeys). If you are in your first term, it is pinned in the sidebar under Drive. Everyone else finds it in the Lab area. You land on your own domain, and tabs along the top switch between domains. If nothing has been published yet, the page says so.

On a domain page, journeys are grouped as **Continue where you left off**, a recommended one at the top, **Not started yet**, and **Completed**.

## Work through a journey

Open a journey to see its map. Each module is one of three states:

- **Completed**
- **Ready to start**: all of its prerequisites are done
- **Locked**: finish the modules it depends on first

Click a module on the map to see what is inside, or click the **Continue** button to jump to the next ready one. The header shows how many modules you have done. **Reset** clears your progress for that journey after you confirm.

In a module you can work through the lesson, then a lab, then a quiz, if the module has them. Buttons walk you through: **Open the lab**, **Take the quiz** and **Mark module complete**, and after a lab or quiz, **Continue to quiz** or **Complete module**. Finishing a module unlocks the modules that depended on it. Finishing the last one shows **Journey complete**.

A quiz has a passing score the author set. Some labs are design labs that you **Submit for review** (**Resubmit for review** after changes are requested). A reviewer approves your work or requests changes. The module completes after approval.

## Build a journey

Core can create a journey in any domain, and domain leads in their own domains. On a domain page, click **New [domain] journey**. It starts as a private draft you own.

In the editor:

- Click **Add module** to add a module. Drag from one module's right dot onto another to make a prerequisite. Click a connection and press Delete to remove it.
- Click a module to edit its details, pages, slides, lab and quiz.
- Changes save as a draft. Learners see them only after you click **Publish**. If something needs fixing first, Publish stays disabled and the issues are listed.
- **Preview** shows the draft as a learner would see it, with every module open.
- **Discard** drops unpublished changes. **Delete journey** removes it for learners along with their progress.

## Share editing

Click **Access** on a journey to add people. An **Owner** can edit, delete and manage access. A person with **Can edit** can edit and publish but cannot delete the journey or change who has access. Core can manage every journey.

## Reviews

Core, and anyone who owns or can edit at least one journey, sees a **Reviews** button on the domain page, with a count of work waiting. [Reviews](/journeys/reviews) lists submitted design lab work, with **Waiting** and **Reviewed** filters. Open one to read it, then click **Approve** or **Request changes**. Core sees reviews for all journeys, and everyone else sees reviews for the journeys they own or can edit. Your own submissions never appear in your queue.
