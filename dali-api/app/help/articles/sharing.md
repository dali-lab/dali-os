Who can open a document depends first on where it lives, and then on sharing you add on top. A document in a project can be read and commented on by any lab member, and edited by the project's team. A document in My Drive is private to you. A document in General is open to the lab. Moving an item into another drive changes its audience.

## Share with people and groups

Open a document, form or whiteboard and click **Share** in the top bar. If you don't see it, you can't manage that document's sharing.

1. Type a name in **Add people by name**, or pick a group under **Or add a group**.
2. Choose a role for them.
3. Click **Done**.

Roles:

- **Can view**: read only.
- **Can comment**: read and comment.
- **Can edit**: edit the document.
- **Full access**: edit and manage sharing.

The owner is pinned at the top of the list. Use the remove button on a row to take someone off.

Who can manage sharing: the owner (for My Drive), the creator and Core (for General), Core and project members (for project documents), Core and instructors (for offering documents), and anyone with Full access. Documents shared with you by name show up under **Shared with me** in [Drive](/drive).

## General access

The **General access** row sets the default audience. In most places the options are:

- **Restricted**: only the people and groups you added.
- **Anyone in the lab**: any lab member with the link. Partners and applicants don't count. A role menu next to it sets what they can do: **Can view**, **Can comment** or **Can edit**.
- **Anyone with the link**: anyone on the internet can read it without an account. They can't edit or comment.

For documents in General, the labels read **Only people you add**, **Everyone in the lab** (new ones start as **Can edit**) and **Anyone with the link**.

Choosing a public link asks you to confirm first. Public pages are read only. The top-level Core, Hiring and Mentorship folders can't be made public, and they stay shared with a group that includes Core.

Click **Copy link** to copy the document's address.

## Folder access

For a folder, **Folder access** sets who can open it and everything inside. Choose **Only people you add**, **Everyone in the lab** or **A group…** and pick the group, such as Core. Everything inside inherits this, though you can still share an item individually.

## Share an uploaded file

An uploaded file, such as a PDF or spreadsheet, can be shared by name like a document. In [Drive](/drive), right-click the file or open its menu and choose **Share…**. If you don't see it, or the dialog says you can't manage it, you don't manage that file's sharing.

The dialog works like the one for documents, with a few differences:

- A row at the top reads **People with access to** the folder, project or the Lab the file lives in. Those people can already open the file, and you can't change that here. Files in My Drive don't have this row.
- Roles are **Can view** (open and download), **Can comment**, **Can edit** (upload new versions, rename and delete) and **Full access** (edit and manage sharing).
- **General access** is **Only people you add** or **Everyone in the lab**. Files can't be made public.

Sharing a file only adds access. A person who already has more access through the folder keeps it, and removing a share never takes away access they get from where the file lives. Because a share applies to the file itself, someone can open a shared file even when they can't open the folder it's in.

The people who manage a file's sharing are the owner (for My Drive), anyone who can edit it through its folder, project or the Lab, and anyone with Full access. People you share with get a notification, and the file shows under **Shared with me** in Drive.

## Partners

On a project document, if the project has an active partner, a **Partners** section appears with **Visible to partners on this project**. Turn it on to let partner accounts open and comment on it in the partner portal.

## Tags

Tags are lab-wide labels. Tags show below a document's title. Only Core sees the tag controls, to add or remove tags and create new ones, and only on project and General documents. Everyone else sees the tags read-only.

In [Drive](/drive), use the tag filter to see everything with a tag, across drives.
