The Core area has two tabs for Core's own work. **Taskboard** is a board of Core's tasks. **Milestones** is the plan for a term, week by week. Only Core members can see them, and any Core member can edit everything.

## Taskboard

Open **Taskboard** at [/core](/core). It shows Core's tasks in five columns: Backlog, To do, In progress, In review and Done. Drag a card to change its status. Select a card to open the task, where you can add comments, a checklist, files, assignees and a due date. Use **New task** to add one, and the filters above the board to narrow it by assignee or search.

You can also add a Core task from a doc that isn't in a project. Type **/task** and pick **Core** as the project. The owner list shows this term's Core members.

## Milestones and plan items

Open **Milestones** at [/core/milestones](/core/milestones). Each week of a term can hold two kinds of items. Neither has a status or a checkbox.

- **Milestones** are lab-wide goals, like "Winter applications open".
- **Plan items** are placeholders for things to do that week, like "Open hiring".

A week can have none, one or several of each.

## Read the term

The term name at the top has arrows to step to the term before or after, summer included. The row of cards below it shows weeks 0 to 10. Week 0 is the week before the term starts. Each card shows the week's start date, its milestones and how many plan items it has. The current week has an outline. Select a card to open that week below it.

The panel shows the week's milestones and plan items. The side cards show what the same week looked like last year and the next two weeks' milestones.

If you step past the last term, the page says that term isn't set up yet. An admin has to create it first.

## Add and edit items

- Select **Add a milestone** or **Add to plan**, type a title and press Enter. In the plan, Enter starts the next item.
- Select a title to change it. Clear the title and leave the field to delete the item.
- Pick owners from the people list. Leave it as **Whole lab** when nobody in particular owns it. Only Core members for that term can be owners.
- Open the **⋯** menu and choose **Add notes** to write notes. They save as you type.

## Reorder and move

- Drag an item by its handle to reorder it within its section.
- Drag an item onto another week's card to move it to the end of the same section there.
- In the **⋯** menu, choose **Move to week…** to pick a week, or **Make it a milestone** or **Make it a plan item** to switch its kind. It lands at the end of the other section.
- **Delete** removes the item.

## Fill from last year

When a term has nothing planned, **Fill from last year** copies milestones and plan items, with their titles, owners and order, from the same season last year. Notes are left empty. It only fills weeks that have nothing yet, so running it again adds nothing. Choose **Start blank** to plan from scratch instead.
