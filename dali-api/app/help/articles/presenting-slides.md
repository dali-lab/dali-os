You can present a slide deck from the browser and let everyone who has the deck open follow along on their own screen.

## Present a deck

Open the deck and choose **Present** at the top right. It starts from the slide you are on and fills the window.

- Press the right arrow, Space or Page Down, or click the slide, to go forward. The left arrow goes back.
- Press Home or End to jump to the first or last slide.
- Press Esc to stop presenting. You go back to the editor.

Open the arrow beside **Present** for more choices: **From the beginning**, **Presenter view**, and **Copy follow link**.

Only people who can edit a deck can present it.

## Use the presenter view

**Presenter view** shows the current slide, the next slide, your speaker notes, a timer and the time of day. Keep it on your laptop and put the slide show on the screen.

- Choose **Presenter view** from the **Present** menu. In a browser it opens in a new tab.
- In the desktop app it opens in the same tab. Use **Open slide show** at the top to switch to the full window slide show.
- Use **A-** and **A+** to change the size of your notes, and **Reset** to restart the timer.
- Choose **End** to stop presenting for everyone.

## Follow along

While someone is presenting, people with the deck open follow along on their own screen. A chip at the bottom shows who is presenting and which slide they are on.

- If you can only view the deck, you follow automatically.
- If you can edit the deck, a bar at the top says who is presenting. Choose **Follow** to join. You are never moved without asking.
- Choose **Browse on my own** to look around the deck yourself. Choose **Follow** to rejoin.

## Ask the audience with a poll

Choose **Insert**, then **Poll**, to add a question to a slide. Select it and use the inspector to write the question, add or remove options (two to six), and choose whether people can pick more than one answer.

- **Voting**: when you present, everyone following sees the poll on their own screen and can tap an option. Each person has one vote per poll, and they can change it until voting closes. Tap the chosen option again to take the vote back.
- **Results**: bars and counts update live on the slide, and in presenter view.
- **Control**: in presenter view, each poll on the current slide has **Close voting** (and **Reopen voting**) and **Reset**, which clears its votes.
- **Fresh start**: votes and closed polls reset each time you start presenting.
- **Join to vote**: on a slide with a poll, a small card in the corner of the slide show and presenter view shows a QR code, **Scan to vote** and the link to the deck. People scan it, sign in if needed, and vote from their phone. Hide it with the X on the card, or with **Show join code** in presenter view. It isn't shown on the screens of people following along.
- **Downloads**: PDF and PowerPoint downloads show the question and options, without votes.

## Reactions

People following your presentation can tap an emoji (thumbs up, clap, heart, laugh, party, thinking) and it floats up the slide on your screen and everyone else's. People can send one a second. Turn **Allow reactions** off in presenter view to hide them for everyone. Reactions aren't saved.

## Make things appear one step at a time

Select a text box, image, chart, table or other item on a slide and open the **Text** tab in the inspector. Under **Appears**, choose **With slide** or a step number. A small badge on the slide shows which step it belongs to.

For a text box, turn on **Bullets one at a time**. The box appears with its first bullet, then each key press adds one more, so three bullets take three presses.

### Set the order and the effect

On the **Slide** tab, the **Builds** list shows every item that appears in steps, in the order it appears.

- Drag an item to change the order. The steps renumber for you, and one undo puts them back.
- Choose how each item starts: **On click**, **With previous** (at the same step as the item above) or **After previous** (it plays by itself, with an optional delay of up to 5 seconds).
- Choose an effect: **Fade** (the default), **Rise**, **Scale** or **Appear**. Items fade in unless you pick something else.
- Click an item in the list to select it on the slide, and the other way round.
- Use the X on a row to take the item out of the builds.

When you present, the forward keys go through the steps of a slide before moving to the next slide. The presenter view shows **Step 2 of 3**. In the editor and in the filmstrip, everything shows.

Steps set to **After previous** play by themselves after the item before them, and followers see them at the same time you do. Pressing a forward key during the wait skips it. Going back skips over automatic steps, so back takes the slide to where you last pressed a key. If your device is set to reduce motion, items appear with no animation.

PDF downloads show everything on each slide. PowerPoint downloads don't include the order things appear in.

## Play a video

A video on a slide shows as a card with a play button. Click it while presenting to play the video in place. Clicking it does not move to the next slide. Press the forward keys to carry on when it is done.

## Transitions

A slide with a transition plays it as you arrive at it, for you and for people following along. The forward keys then run the slide's build steps. If your device is set to reduce motion, slides change with no animation.
