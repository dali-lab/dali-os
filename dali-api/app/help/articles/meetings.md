A meeting is a calendar event with guests from DALI OS. DALI OS tells the guests and gives the meeting its own page with notes and attendance. To also send Google invites, invite people by email or add a Google Meet link, you need a linked Google account with a calendar you can write to. See [Calendar](/help/articles/calendar).

## Schedule a meeting

1. On [Calendar](/calendar), click **Add event**, or click or drag on an empty spot in the Week or Day view.
2. Type a title.
3. Add guests: other members or a group, or an email address if you have a linked Google account. As soon as you add a guest the form becomes a meeting and an availability grid opens on the left.
4. Pick a time (see below), then set **Repeat**, **Send invite from**, **Location** and **Description** if you need them.
5. Under **Add to this meeting**, choose **Google Meet**, **Meeting notes**, **Whiteboard**, or **Add to timesheet**.
6. Click **Create meeting**. You see how many people were notified.

On a project's board, **New**, then **Meeting** opens the same form with the project's group already invited.

Typing a room name in **Location** can hold that room for the meeting. See [Rooms](/help/articles/rooms).

## Find a time

With guests added, the grid on the left shows each person's free time for the week, built from their working hours and linked Google calendars. Click or drag a free stretch to fill in the date and time. The week arrows move forward and back.

**Best times** shows up to a few suggested slots ranked by how many guests are free, marked **All free** or something like **3/4 free**. Click one to use it. Guests who have not linked a Google calendar are listed under **No calendar** because their availability is unknown. A guest invited by email shows no availability.

If there is no good slot, schedule at the closest time. Guests can answer with **Propose new time**.

## Answer an invite

When someone invites you, the invite appears in the bell and on My Tasks with **Going**, **Maybe** and **Can't make it**. You can also click the event on the calendar and answer there with **Going**, **Maybe** or **Can't make it**. Your answer goes to Google Calendar too.

To suggest another time, click **Propose new time** on the event, pick a date and time, and confirm. The organizer is notified. The organizer or Core then sees a **Proposed times** section on the meeting page and chooses **Accept** or **Decline**. Accepting moves the meeting. Organizers can't propose a time for their own meeting.

## The meeting page

Click a meeting on the calendar and open it to get its page. The header has the buttons that apply:

- **Join Google Meet**, if the meeting has a link.
- **Open meeting note**, **Transcript** and **Open whiteboard**. See [Meeting notes](/help/articles/meeting-notes).
- **Edit event**, for the organizer and Core.

Below that you see when, who organized it, where, and the **Notebook** its notes go into. For a repeating meeting, the arrows next to the time move between occurrences. Each occurrence has its own notes and its own attendance.

Only invited people, the organizer, Core and members of the meeting's project can open a meeting page. Lab-wide meetings can be opened by any lab member.

## Edit or cancel

Only the organizer and Core can edit or cancel a meeting.

- **Edit**: on the meeting page click **Edit event**. You can change the title, guests, time, location and description, then **Save changes**. On a repeating meeting, an edit from this form applies to the whole series.
- **Cancel**: open the event on the calendar and click **Delete**, or use **Event options**, then **Cancel event**, on the [Attendance](/attendance) page. Guests are told and it can't be undone. For a repeating meeting you choose how much to cancel.
- A Google event that was only **Track in DALI OS** can be untracked with **Stop tracking**. The Google event stays.

## Check in

Meetings keep an attendance list for everyone invited. People can be marked present in three ways: the organizer, Core or a project member marks them on the meeting page; people check themselves in if self check-in is on; or the organizer, Core or a project member scans their wallet pass. See [Attendance](/help/articles/attendance) for the details.
