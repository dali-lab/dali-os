Ask DALI answers questions in plain language. It looks through DALI docs, meetings, tasks, people, projects and these help articles, then writes a short answer with a link to each source. Use it for things like "What am I on the hook for this week?", "What did we decide in my last meeting?" or "How do I request a room for an event?".

## Open Ask DALI

There are three ways in:

- Press **⌘J** (Ctrl+J on Windows and Linux) from any page. Press it again to close the panel.
- Select **Ask** in the page bar at the top of the page.
- Press **⌘K** to open search, type your question, and choose the **Ask DALI** row. If what you typed reads like a question, the row appears at the top. Otherwise it is at the bottom.

Ask DALI opens as a panel beside the page, so you can keep working. If you don't see these, it hasn't been turned on for you yet.

## Ask a question

Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter. Use Shift and Enter for a new line. On a blank panel you can also select one of the example questions under **Try asking**.

While it works, you see short steps like "Searching", "Reading a meeting" or "Checking your tasks". Select **Stop** to cancel an answer in progress.

After the answer you can **Ask a follow-up** in the same box. It remembers the earlier questions in the panel. Select **Start over** at the top of the panel to clear the conversation and begin again.

## Sources

Below each answer, **Sources** lists what the answer came from, numbered to match the numbers in the text. Each row shows the kind of thing it is, such as a document, meeting, transcript, task, project, person or help page, with a date where there is one. Select a row to open it.

## What it can and can't do

- It only reads. It can't create tasks, send messages, book rooms or change anything. Ask it how, and it points you to the page or help article that does.
- It only sees what you can already open. It won't show you a document you don't have access to or a meeting you weren't invited to.
- The answer is written by AI. The panel says "Written by AI from DALI docs, meetings and tasks" at the bottom. It can be wrong or miss something.

## Daily limit

Each person can ask a limited number of questions per day. When you are close to the limit, the panel shows how many are left today. When you reach it, you see "You've used today's questions" and the time you can ask again. Search still works as usual. If an answer fails because of an error, the question isn't counted. Select **Try again** to ask it again.
