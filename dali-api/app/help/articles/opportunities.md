Opportunities is a feed in Community where alumni share jobs and anyone can ask for a coffee chat. Open it from [Opportunities](/community/opportunities).

## Share a job

Alumni and Core can share a job. Select **Share**, choose **Job**, and paste a link to the posting. DALI OS fills in the company, role, location and type, and shows the company's logo when it finds one. Check the details, turn on **I'll refer DALI students** if you will, write a message, and select **Post**.

Students can't share jobs, but they can see them and select **I'm interested**. You can add a short note, and the person who shared the job gets a notification with a link to your profile. They reply to you by email or Slack.

## Ask for a coffee chat

Anyone can ask for a chat. Select **Share**, pick a topic or type your own, and write what you would like to talk about. Alumni who can help select **Offer a chat**. You both get an email introducing you to each other, so reply to it to find a time.

An ask shows up pinned at the top for people it matches:
- The topic is on their **Ask me about** list and they are open to coffee chats.
- The topic is **Resume review** and they are open to resume reviews, or **Portfolio review** and they are open to portfolio reviews.

Matching ignores case and extra spaces and doesn't guess at similar topics. A topic nobody listed shows in the feed for everyone and is pinned for no one. You can turn on notifications for asks that match you in your notification settings. They are off by default.

## Is this still open?

A month after you share a job, DALI OS asks whether it is still open. Select **Still open** to keep it in the feed, or **Filled** to close it. If you don't answer, the job closes about a week later. You can also close your own job or ask any time from its menu with **Mark as filled** or **Mark as done**.

## Refer a partner

Referrals for project partners don't go in this feed. Use [Refer a partner](/refer). The status of your referrals shows on the side of the Opportunities page.

## Filter the feed

Use **Show** to see Everything, Jobs or Coffee chats. You can delete your own posts and comments, and Core can delete any.
