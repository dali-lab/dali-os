Every term, DALI places members onto projects. You tell staffing leads what you plan to do through a few forms, and Core members place people on the staffing board. This article covers what you fill out and what happens next. If you are on Core and run the process, see [Running staffing](/help/articles/running-staffing).

## Terms and levels

DALI works in terms, named with a year and a season, like 26S. Each term has its own staffing round.

Within a domain (for example Design or Development) you have a level:

- **P1** is a learner.
- **P2** is a doer.
- **P3** is a mentor.

Your level is set per domain, and it is the highest level you can be staffed at in that domain. Someone can be P3 in one domain and P1 in another. Core members change levels, usually after a level up request (see below). A P3 on a project is shown as a mentor by default.

## Intent to work

The Intent to Work form tells staffing leads whether you will be around for a term. Your answer for each term is one of:

- **Returning**: you plan to work.
- **Off this term**: you will not be working.
- **Graduating**.
- **On leave**.
- **Unsure**.

If you are off for a term (leave, a D-Plan off term, or you are graduating), say so here. Staffing leads use these answers to plan the roster. You can fill the form again later and your newest answer replaces the old one.

## Project bids

On the Project Bids form you rank the projects you would most like to work on. You can rank up to three, with your first choice first. A few things to know:

- If you pick the same project twice, only the higher-ranked pick counts.
- You can bid on any project. Your level in a domain does not block a bid.
- Bids are not a guarantee. They are what staffing leads look at when they place you.
- Only Core members and Admins can see bids.

## Level up requests

If you want to move up a level in a domain, such as P1 to P2, fill out the Level Up form for the term. You say which domain and which level you are applying for. A Core member reviews the request and, if approved, updates your level.

## Where the forms are

Staffing leads connect one form for each of Intent to Work, Project Bids and Level Up every term, then send it to the lab through an announcement. Look for the announcement or email with the link, or a form to-do. For some forms, you will be sent to fill them out before you can use the rest of the app, like an unsigned agreement. You can only fill a locked form once. A form can also be closed. If you can't open one, ask a Core member.

## How placement works

After the forms close, Core members open the staffing board for the term. It has a column for each project and an **Unassigned** column for people who are not placed yet. They look at your intent, your bids and your levels, then drag each person onto a project. A Core member can also add someone who did not bid.

Placing you on the board does not tell you yet. Your placement becomes official when a Core member finalizes the project. Finalizing a project:

- Confirms the proposed placements.
- Can post the roster to Slack, set up the project's GitHub team, and create the project's team email group.

When your placement is confirmed, you get a **Staffing assignments** notification with your project, domain and level. It links to the project page. You can turn this notification on or off in your notification settings.

After that, Core issues the term's agreements to everyone staffed. You will get a request to sign, so watch for it. See [Running staffing](/help/articles/running-staffing) for the order Core follows.

## If you disagree with a placement

Tell a Core member as early as you can. Moving someone before the project is finalized is easier than after.
