The first time you sign in as an alum, DALI OS invites you to a short welcome flow. It takes about two minutes and ends on a page of places worth a look. If you choose **Maybe later**, the invite comes back next time you open DALI OS.

## Step 1: Check your details

[Welcome back](/alumni/onboarding) fills in your class, hometown, DALI projects and roles, and your current and previous job from what DALI already knows. Choose **Edit** on a row to fix it in place. Your DALI projects and roles come from lab records, so **Edit** on those rows opens your [profile](/profile). Choose **Looks good** to move on, or **Skip for now**.

## Step 2: Interests

Add a fun fact, pick the topics people can ask you about (or add your own), and say what you do outside of work. A few words each is fine.

## Step 3: What are you open to?

Turn on what you are happy to help with: coffee chats, resume reviews, partner referrals, mentoring a project and speaking at a lab event. Students and DALI staff only ask you for what you turn on. **Resume reviews** covers both resumes and portfolios.

## Change it later

Everything from the flow lives on your [profile](/profile). **Alumni details** holds your topics, fun fact, outside of work and what you are open to, and you can edit them any time.
