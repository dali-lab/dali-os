The first time you sign in as an alum, DALI OS invites you to finish your alumni profile. It takes about two minutes and ends on a page of places worth a look. When the pixel lab is on for you, the invite offers two ways to do it: **Fill it in** opens the three short steps below, and **Walk the pixel lab** does the same thing by chatting with Tim. Finishing either one counts. If you choose **Later**, the invite comes back next time you open DALI OS.

**Finish later** in the top corner of every step takes you back to DALI OS. Anything you already saved stays saved.

## Step 1: Check your details

[Welcome back](/alumni/onboarding) fills in what DALI already knows. Choose **Edit** (or **Add**) on a row to change it in place:

- **Class**: your class year, majors and minors. Add more than one major or minor, and select one to remove it.
- **Hometown** and **Based in**. Pick a city from the list so you show up on the Connect map.
- **Email**: the address DALI uses to reach you, shown as **Primary** on your profile. Pick one on file or add another.
- **Now** and **Before that**: your current job and the one before it.

Your DALI projects and roles sit in the **On record at DALI** card and come from lab records, so you can't edit them here. If something is wrong or missing, choose **Anything off?**, describe it and choose **Send**. Core gets a notification and fixes the record.

Choose **Looks good** to move on.

## Step 2: A bit more about you

Everything here is optional. Add a fun fact, pick the topics people can ask you about (or add your own), say what you do outside of work, and add your LinkedIn and personal site. **For DALI events** holds your birthday and any dietary restrictions.

## Step 3: What are you open to?

Turn on what you are happy to help with: coffee chats, resume reviews, partner referrals, mentoring a project and speaking at a lab event. Students and DALI staff only ask you for what you turn on. **Resume reviews** covers both resumes and portfolios.

## Change it later

Everything from the flow lives on your [profile](/profile). **Alumni details** holds your topics, fun fact, outside of work and what you are open to, and you can edit them any time.
