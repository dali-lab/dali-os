Drive is where the lab keeps documents, folders, files, whiteboards, forms and notebooks. Open it from [Drive](/drive) in the sidebar.

## Drives

Drive opens on a list of drives. Which ones you see depends on your role.

- **My Drive**: your private space. Only you can open what is in it.
- **Favorites**: items you starred, from every drive.
- **Shared with me**: documents and files someone shared with you by name.
- **General**: lab-wide documents and files.
- **Projects** and **Education**: one space per project or offering you can access.
- **Core**, **Hiring** and **Mentorship**: shown when you have access to them.

**Favorites** and **Shared with me** only list things that live elsewhere, so you can't create, move or delete there. Open the item first.

Click **Drive** at the top of the page to step back out to the list of drives.

## Browse and search

Drive can show folders as columns, a list or a grid. Select an item and press the spacebar for a quick preview of an image or PDF.

Use the **Search Drive** box in the toolbar to search across every drive you can open. While you search, results show where each item lives. The **Filter by type** menu narrows the view to documents, notebooks, whiteboards, files, forms and so on. If the lab has tags, a tag filter also appears; see [Sharing and visibility](/help/articles/sharing).

## Create something

Open a drive or folder, then click **New**.

- **New document**, **New folder**, **New whiteboard**, **New notebook** and **New form** ask for a name, then create the item in the folder you are viewing.
- **New agreement** appears in Core, for people who manage agreements.
- **From template…** starts a document from a template (not offered in My Drive).
- **Upload files** picks files from your computer. You can also drag files into the folder.

**New** is greyed out on the list of drives, in **Favorites** and in **Shared with me**. Open a drive or folder first. Notebooks need a project or offering to live in.

## Whiteboards

**New whiteboard** creates a freeform canvas you can pan and zoom, with shapes, text and images. Several people can draw on it at once. It is shared and moved like a document. **Full screen** and **Share** are in the top bar. Click the board's name at the top of the page or in the breadcrumb to rename it. Hover over the name to add an icon, or click the icon to change it.

## Move and rename

Right-click an item, or use its menu, for **Open**, **Download** (files only), **Rename**, **Move to…** and **Delete**. Documents, folders, forms and files also offer **Share…**. To share a file, see [Sharing and visibility](/help/articles/sharing). You can drag an item onto a folder to move it, and select several items to move or delete them together.

Moving an item into another drive changes who can see it, because it takes on that drive's audience. The move dialog tells you who that will be.

Core, Hiring and other process folders at the top of a drive can't be deleted.

## Download

- One file downloads directly: right-click it and choose **Download**.
- Several files: select them and click **Download** in the bar at the bottom. Drive builds a ZIP of the files. Anything that isn't a file you can view is left out, and Drive tells you how many.
- Documents aren't downloaded from Drive. Open the document and use its menu; see [Documents](/help/articles/documents).

## Trash and restore

**Delete** moves an item to Trash and shows an undo option. To get something back, click the **More Drive actions** button (three dots) and choose **Trash**. Each row has **Restore**, and a button to delete it permanently. Permanent deletion asks you to confirm and can't be undone.

You only see trashed items you could have opened.

## Templates

**Templates** in the same three-dot menu opens [Templates](/drive/templates). It lists **Your templates** (only you see these), lab document templates, and project templates (Core only). Click a card to start from it. A template of yours creates the new document in your space, and a lab template creates it in General. To make a document a template, open it, use its three-dot menu and choose **Mark as template**.
