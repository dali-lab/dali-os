Attendance tracks who showed up. Each meeting or event that tracks attendance keeps a list of everyone invited and whether each person was checked in. The [Attendance](/attendance) page, under Lab in the sidebar, collects those lists in one place. Alumni do not see it.

## What the Attendance page shows

The page lists events you organize or are invited to. Core members see the same list as everyone else, not every event in the lab. Events are split into **Upcoming** and **Past**, and older ones load as you scroll to the bottom.

- Search by event, organizer, project or attendee name. Use the sort menu to order the names inside each list.
- Each card shows the event name, date, organizer and a bar with **checked in / invited**, such as 8/12.
- Click a card to open its lists. **Checked in** shows each person with the time they checked in. **Not submitted** shows everyone who has not.
- The clipboard button (**Check in or mark attendance**) opens the meeting page.
- Organizers and Core also get **Event options**, with **Edit event** and **Cancel event**. Cancelling removes the event for everyone invited, including every date of a repeating event, and can't be undone.

Everyone invited to an event can see who has checked in. Only the organizer, Core and members of the meeting's project can add or read **absence notes**, which explain why someone was out. Use the note button next to a name under **Not submitted**.

## How a check-in counts

Marking someone present does two things. It counts them in the checked-in total for that date, and it adds a time entry of the meeting's length to that person's timesheet. Marking them absent again removes that entry. A repeating meeting keeps a separate list for each date.

Only people who were invited can be marked present. The exception is a self check-in event, where scanning any DALI member's wallet pass adds them.

## Check yourself in

If the organizer turned on self check-in, the meeting page and its check-in link show a **Check in** button. Click it and you are marked present. It reads **Checked in** afterward.

Self check-in works from 15 minutes before the meeting starts until 15 minutes after it ends. Outside that, you see that the check-in window has closed. If you were not invited, there is nothing to check in to. The window only limits self check-in. Organizers, Core and project members can mark people present or scan passes at any time.

Organizers, Core and project members see a QR code instead of, or as well as, the button. They can open it fullscreen to show a room, open the link, or use **Download PDF** to print it. People scan the code with their phone and check in.

To turn self check-in on for an existing meeting, open the meeting page and click **Turn on self check-in**. You need to be the organizer or Core, and also Core, Admin or an instructor.

## Mark people present

The organizer, Core and project members can mark attendance from the meeting page. Open the meeting from Calendar or from the clipboard button on [Attendance](/attendance). The page shows **X of Y present** and a checklist where you tick each person. Click the sort menu to order names.

You can also scan wallet passes:

- **Scan passes** on the meeting page uses your device's camera. Each scan marks that member present.
- **Enable attendance tracking from iPad** turns every door display into a pass scanner for that event. Only the organizer and Core see it. The page shows **Scanning on iPads** while it is on, and the iPads also show a live check-in count. Only one event can use the iPads at a time. Use **Switch iPads to this event** to take them over, and **Stop attendance tracking from iPad** to end it. See [Rooms](/help/articles/rooms).

Scanning needs wallet passes to be turned on for the lab.

## What other people see

If you are invited and are not an organizer, the meeting page shows **You're marked present for this meeting** or **Your attendance will be marked by the organizer**. You do not see the checklist or other people's notes.
