A [connected AI assistant](/help/articles/ai-assistants) with write access can change part of a page or story without rewriting the rest. It can fix a phrase, update one paragraph, or add a row to a table. Everything else stays as it was, including mentions, inline comments and anything someone else is typing.

An assistant can only edit what you can edit yourself. It works on pages and on stories you wrote.

## What you see while an assistant works

If you have the page or story open, you see the assistant arrive like any other editor.

- Its caret appears under its name, for example **Claude**, in its own colour.
- The blocks it just changed light up in that colour, then fade after a few seconds.
- It can point at text without changing anything, to show you what it is looking at.
- On a page, it also appears in the bar of people viewing the page. The card says whose assistant it is and whether it is reading, editing or pointing.

If you run two assistant sessions on the same page, they show as **Claude** and **Claude 2**, each in its own colour. An assistant drops out of view about a minute after it last did something.

## Undo an assistant's change

Every change an assistant makes is saved in the page's version history. Open **Version history** to see an earlier version or restore it.
