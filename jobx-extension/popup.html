DALI → JobX settings

On a JobX Manage Time Sheet page, click the green “Fill from DALI” button. It pulls your finalized period from DALI (you must be logged into DALI in this browser) and fills the form. It never submits — you click Save.

Required. Local dev: http://localhost:3001 — prod: https://os.dali.dartmouth.edu

Blank = the current finalized period.
