Typography, color, shape and components
RoundedCards and dialogs take a 24px corner, items 12px, fields 10px. Every button and chip is a full pill.
BlueGrounds read as cool paper, ink is the brand navy, and there is one accent. Coral and teal never mean a second thing.
BigPage titles at 36px, section titles at 19px, body and controls at 14px. Panels get 24px of padding.
Quiet chromeCards carry no border and no shadow. The ground does the work. Only popovers and dialogs cast a shadow.
Both modesEvery token has a light and a dark value. Anything built from tokens works in both for free. Check both.

Monochrome line icons in the secondary grey, going to the primary ink on hover. 16px in controls, 17px in detail rows, 14px in eyebrows. Never a colored icon to mean a state.

Copy

Sentence case for titles and buttons. Uppercase only comes from eyebrow and caption styles.
Label first. If a control needs explaining, put it in a tooltip, not helper text under the label.
Verbs on confirms. Delete, Remove, Revoke. Never a bare Confirm.
No em dashes in UI strings. Use a period or a comma. A bare dash as an empty table cell is fine.
No marketing openings. Say the thing. "Start drawing. Everyone on this board sees your changes live."
No second accent. Coral and teal don't appear in the shell to mean a second thing.
